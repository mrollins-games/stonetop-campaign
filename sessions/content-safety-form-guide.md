## Form Settings

- **Title:** Stonetop — Content Preferences
- **Description:** "Before session zero, I'd like everyone to share any content you want handled carefully, excluded entirely, or just kept off-screen. This is anonymous — I'll compile results and share a combined list without names. You don't need to explain your reasons."
- **Anonymous:** ✅ (Settings → Who can fill out this form → "Anyone can respond" + uncheck "Record name")

---

## Questions

### Q1 — Choice (Rating Grid)
**Title:** "For each topic below, how would you like it handled?"

**Scale definitions** (include these at the top of Q1 as helper text):

> - **Fine as-is** — This topic can appear in the game on-screen. Described, role-played, consequences shown.
> - **Veil it** — This can exist in the story but only off-camera. We can say "it happened" or reference it, but we don't describe it or play it out. Fade to black.
> - **Exclude it** — This doesn't exist in our game world at all. Not threatened, not referenced, not in backstories. It simply isn't part of the fiction.

| Topic | Fine as-is | Veil it (off-camera only) | Exclude it entirely |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Graphic violence / gore | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Harm to children | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Kidnapping / abduction | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Bigotry / racial tension | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Horror / body horror | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Mind control / possession | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Personal corruption / moral decay | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Starvation / resource desperation | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Torture | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Sexual violence | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Animal cruelty | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Suicide / self-harm | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Insects / spiders (described in detail) | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Deep water / drowning | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| Claustrophobia / being trapped | ○ | ○ | ○ |

> **Forms how-to:** Use "Likert" question type. Rows = topics, Columns = Fine / Veil / Exclude.

### Q5 — Choice (single)
**Title:** "If something comes up during play that bothers you, what's your preferred way to flag it?"
**Options:**
- Say "time out" openly in the call
- Private message the GM in Teams chat
- Use a code word (we'll pick one together)
- I'll figure it out in the moment

---

## After Collecting Responses

1. Review all responses
2. If ANY player says "Exclude" → it's excluded (most restrictive wins)
3. If one says "Veil" and no one says "Exclude" → it's veiled
4. Compile into a single anonymous list
5. Present at session zero during Phase 3 (Content Safety)
6. Record in `steading/stonetop.md` or the session-zero checklist under Content Safety
