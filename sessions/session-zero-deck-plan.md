## Design Notes

- **Art Style:** Match the Stonetop game books — hand-drawn ink illustrations, woodcut-inspired, earthy and grounded. Use artwork extracted from the PDFs or AI-generated in the same style.
- **Background:** Off-white / warm parchment (#F5F0E8 or similar) — NOT dark theme
- **Every slide** should have some art element (book illustration, generated piece, or decorative border)
- **Text:** Minimal — you're talking over these. Use readable serif or semi-serif font.
- **Layout:** Clean, generous whitespace. Art should support, not compete with text.
- **Art/layout discussion:** Defer to end — finalize content first, then do art pass

- **Total:** ~27 slides (may shift as we finalize remaining phases)
- **Build time:** ~2 hours with assets gathered
