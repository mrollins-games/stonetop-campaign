## Design Notes

- **Theme:** Dark/muted with warm accents (worn parchment + firelight)
- **Total:** ~16 slides — mostly full-bleed images and maps
- **Text:** Minimal — just titles on section breaks. You're talking over these, not reading them.
- **Maps:** Full-slide, no overlay clutter
- **Build time:** ~1 hour with assets gathered
