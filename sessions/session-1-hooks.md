## Recommended First Session Picks

**Best "both" option:** Start with **#3 (Cistern Whispers)** as the internal tension that leads to **#11 (Swyn's Collection)** — the cistern sounds are echoes of the swyn's psychic activity in nearby Maker-ruins. This gives internal mystery → external expedition in one arc, touches all 5 playbooks, and uses the book's suggested first-adventure framework.

**Pure internal start:** **#8 (Lightning Omen)** — engages every PC's god/arcana relationship, builds mystery, can branch to any external hook next session.

**Pure external start:** **#12 (Logging Party)** — immediate stakes, nearby, simple premise, gives the Would-Be Hero a rescue to charge into and the Seeker a barrow to investigate.
