## Session Summary

The group gathered for a four-hour session zero to establish content safety agreements, create characters, build the village of Stonetop, and define relationships. The session was played over Microsoft Teams.

Players collaboratively built out the village: assigning homes on the map, defining NPCs and their occupations, establishing inter-PC relationships and bonds, and laying the groundwork for emerging story hooks. The session flowed through the standard Stonetop session-zero procedure — introductions in rounds, each player adding NPCs, answering relationship questions, and placing homes on the steading map.
