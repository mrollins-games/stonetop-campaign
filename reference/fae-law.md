# Fae Law

> "The fae or nah, the following is law"

## The Laws

1. **Words have meaning** — but Homophones, Homonyms & Homographs are valid substitutes
2. **That which is spoken — is** — speech creates reality among the Fae
3. **Not every babbling fool can speak things into existence** — yet in the right conditions, if they ring true, they can become so
4. **Scales yearn for balance** — an agreement is upheld or enforced
5. **Breaking tradition, rites, or rituals that were expected leave the scales unbalanced** — custom carries the weight of law
6. **Essence of things create them and they create their essence** — circular causality; a thing is what it is because it is

---

## Tips from Experience

*Scribbled in the margins...*

- **Breaking custom is shocking and beyond obscene** — no quicker way to lose favor and invite ire
- **Ask "would you permit me?"** — a good way to avoid accidentally becoming indebted when receiving hospitality or observing customs. It keeps the scales balanced when you hold the permit to receive without debt. Though let permits unreturned pile up...
- **A complement in return may suffice** — when offered tea, it's rude to say no, but being in debt is not ideal. Complementing the host's generosity verbosely IS the return gift

---

## GM Application Notes

These laws govern ALL Fae interactions. When running Fae encounters:
- Every gift creates debt unless explicitly permitted or reciprocated
- Words spoken carelessly can become binding truths
- Puns, double-meanings, and wordplay are legitimate tools — the Fae exploit these constantly
- Customs once established cannot be casually broken without consequence
- The *essence* principle means names have power, descriptions can create, and identity is mutable
