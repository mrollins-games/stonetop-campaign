281  There’s a cave in the   Great Wood   (page 200), a few hours north of Stonetop. No underbrush grows within 30 feet of it, and bones litter the entrance. Trappers and hunters avoid it, and claim to have heard strange noises coming from its direction— especially at night.  Impressions  An unnatural quiet, the forest holding its breath  A blighted patch, spreading out from  the cave, where only grey lichen grows  Bones strewn about, broken & chewed  The black void of the cave mouth  Those strange noises from within Tip: speak softly, hold very still. Pause a little longer between words, sentences.  Questions  Who among you have been here before? What brought you?  What do the noises from the Maw  (supposedly) sound like?  What do you think is in there? Why?  Hooks  A beloved animal goes missing; some- one saw it near the Maw.  A hunter reports strange figures (or tracks) coming and going.  A sickness afflicts the town; the treatment is an herb that grows near the Maw, but that must be harvested under moonlight.  What could be in there?  That’s for you to decide, but perhaps it’s... …   a   hagr   (page 208), afflicted with some withering curse; … a brutal, gluttonous   Fae   (page 94), and an entrance to its   domain   (page 102); …   an idol or emanation of the   Things Below   (page 412); …   a passage to one of the   Fomoraij ’s (page 136) twisted realms; …   a   specter   (page 79) or a pack of  wraiths   (page 79), terrible and ancient and only partly bound; or …   something else entirely.
