258  There and back again  A typical trip to and from Lygos involves:  An entire season of travel (80-90 days), there and back.  Joining a merchant caravan for the trip there, and another one for the trip back (Value 2 each way).  While in Lygos, either…  …   a purse of silvers (Value 2) to pay for room, board, and bribes; or …   a resident of Lygos willing to put you up and cover your costs.  The risk of misadventure, robbery, betrayal, or sundry other setbacks at every turn. Don’t play out trips to Lygos in detail! Doing so would take dozens of sessions and could easily derail your campaign. Unless you and your group actively decide to leave Stonetop behind and play a very different sort of game, then you should handle PC trips to Lygos off-camera. Use   love letters   ( Book I , page 568) to gloss over such a journey. Work with the play- er(s) to identify:  What they hope to accomplish  How they plan to go about it  What valuables/resources they bring with them Think about what might go wrong, what might go well, and what obligations or simmering troubles they’re leaving behind. Think about elements of the setting or character that you want to explore or highlight. Weave these together into a love letter for each PC who makes the journey, and present it to each player when their PC returns to Stonetop. If some PCs remained behind while the others went to Lygos, you can write love letters for the PCs who stayed behind, summarizing events back home. Or, you can play one or more sessions with just those players. Or you can replace the trav- elling PCs with temporary PCs for a few sessions. Work with your group to decide on the best approach.
