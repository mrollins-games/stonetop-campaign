449  Titan Bones Titan Bones  About two days’ hike from Stonetop stands a lone hill, rising from the   Flats   (page 126). A huge, fossilized skeleton is visible on one side of the hill. The   Highway   (page 270) runs past the hill to the east; a wayside sits at its foot. The hill rises over 300’ above the prairie. The northern, leeward face is steep, rocky, cliff-like. The other sides are rugged but manageable slopes, covered in sparse, gnarled oaks and smaller brush. The crown is flat, sparsely wooded, the highest point for nigh on 100 miles. When Stonetop or the   Hillfolk   (page 228) need to signal each other, they light a great bon- fire up there, on the top of Titan Bones.  Impressions  The hill on the horizon, never seeming to get closer until it’s right up on you  Those bones, bigger than they ought to be, bigger than any beast you’ve seen  A sense of smallness, insignificance  Southerly wind: ceaseless, buffeting  Riders approaching, the anticipation as you wait  Tip:   Up top, portray NPCs with short, loud sentences, like they’re shouting over the wind. Ask, “What?” if players don’t do the same. Down at the wayside, keep look- ing up and away, like NPCs are distracted by the hill.  Questions  Which of you has been (t)here before? Why?  Do the bones peek out of the sheer rock face, or jut from the southern slopes, or lie flat on the crown, or what?  According to the tales, what are they bones of ? How did they come to be here?  What superstitions surround the bones? What little rites do folks ob- serve in their presence?  What other oddities have you found on or around the hill?

450  Titan Bones Titan Bones  Meeting place  A footpath leads from the wayside, climbing the rugged slope in a series of switchbacks. It takes half an hour to reach the crown at a reasonable pace, but a fit person could charge straight up in just a few minutes (at considerable risk of injury or mishap). When the PCs wish to summon the Hill- folk to a meeting, they must:  Travel to Titan Bones and climb the hill  Wait for a clear day, with no rain or haze  Spend the best part of a day gathering fuel and stoking a bonfire  Tend and feed the bonfire all through the night and into the early morn  Wait for the Hillfolk to arrive Choose or roll for   how long   before the Hillfolk arrive, what happens   while they wait , and the   disposition   of the Hillfolk who show up. 451  Titan Bones Titan Bones  The titan’s bones  The fossilized bones on the hill are clearly ancient, and far bigger than anything has a right to be. But what are they the bones of ? That’s for you and your group to de- cide, but perhaps they are the bones of…  …   one of the three great beasts vanquished by   Tor   (page 452); …   one of the   First Creators   (page 299), slain by the   Things Below   (page 412) as they were imprisoned; …   some fell beast brewed up by the  Green Lords   (page 210); …   a mighty   Stone Lord construct   (page 392), not a creature at all; … a colossal   emanation   (page 436) of the Things Below, a world-ending horror,  vitrified by   Stormcatcher ’s (page 332) magic; …   some combination of the above; or …   something else entirely.  Ruins of the past  Today, Titan Bones is little more than a landmark and a meeting place, but what was it in the past? That’s for you to decide, but perhaps it was once… … a site sacred to the   Makers   (page 264), a place of pilgrimage; …   a fortress or trading post of the   Stone Lords   (page 382); …   one of the   Tempest Lords ’ (page 398) sky-islands, crashed or landed and (temporarily?) unable to fly; …   part of   Stormcatcher ’s (page 332) domain: a lookout, a laboratory, a power station, etc.; …   a fortress from the time of the   Barrow Builders   (page 34), likely   haunted  (page 72) from the battles fought here; …   a   corrupted site   (page 422), a place of great evil, now mostly cleansed or at least contained; …   some combination of the above; or …   something else entirely. Whatever you decide, consider how that interacts with your decisions about the titan’s bones (see previous column). For ex- ample, if they are the bones of a great beast and the hill was once a sky-island, did the beast and sky-island bring each other low? Did the beast (or its bones) power the sky-island? Or what?
