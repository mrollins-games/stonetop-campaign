Some are short and wispy, others thick and taller than a tall man. They bloom in a myriad of shapes and colors, and sport every manner of prickle, thorn, and seedpod. The Gwead will not be tamed. Burn the grasses, plow the soil, tear things up by their roots—it doesn’t matter. The Gwead always come back, thicker and hardier than before.
