48  Blackwater Lake  In the west of the   Steplands   (page 372), in the shadow of the   Huffel Peaks   (page 236), there is a large and placid lake. A low ridge surrounds it, and a wide, rocky shore. The Aberzh River flows in from the west. No rivers or streams flow out. The water is dark, and very deep, and very, very still. It is always cold but never freezes. There are no waves. The wind shuns the surface. No birds dare alight. This was once great Dulenn, city of Dark Waters, the slow-beating heart of the  Stone Lords ’ vast domain (page 382). Whereas   Teirstered   (page 438) was a city of towers and splendor, Dulenn was a solemn place. A quiet warren, carved and sung from bedrock with the beauty of a limestone cave. There was no lake, then. Not above the ground. But it was called “Dark Waters” for a reason. For seven deep tunnels led to seven mighty seals, each guarded by Stone Lord sentinels who sang a ceaseless lulling song. Behind those doors was a vast and lightless lake. A hole in the earth. A  Mouth of Daagon   (page 55). When the   Green Lords   (page 210) roused the Things Below to help them quell the   Fae   (page 94), the seals below Dulenn cracked. Daagon began to whisper. Began to sing. Resentment grew among the Stone Lords’ human servants. Leaders rose among them: cunning, confident, and eloquent, knowing things they oughtn’t, awash in uncanny power. Cults of libera- tion formed. A conspiracy grew.
