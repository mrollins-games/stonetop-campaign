28  People  The people of Barrier Pass descend from the   Rime Lords ’ (page 316) mortal disciples. Their names are Nepalese- and Tibetan-inspired (see page 483).  The Three Honorable Sages   are  powerful   adepts   (page 328) who preside over Barrier Pass. Each leads one of the three sects.  The Empty Sky sect   values preser-  vation and stillness; its monks spend much time in meditative stasis. Led by   Gar Torjeng   ( Instinct   to avoid disruption and distraction).  The Unmoved Mountain sect  teaches extreme ascetic disciplines, pushing adherents to continually test themselves. Led by   Preshi Kav   ( Instinct  to drive her students towards perfection).  The White Crystal sect   is devoted  to order. The majority of guardians and layfolk follow this sect. Its few monks often lead patrols, mete out justice in nearby farms, and hunt monsters. Led by  Oparma Div   ( Instinct   to tame chaos).  Monks from each sect   dwell in the  fortress, ranging wildly in experience and aptitude. (~40 total, including novices)  The   Guardians   are sworn to defend  Barrier Pass. Many are failed monks. They bear ancient bronze shields and armor, but wield no edged weapons. (~20 total)  The rest of the inhabitants   are lay  folk: families and individuals who garden, herd, cook, clean, and otherwise keep the fortress-monastery running.
