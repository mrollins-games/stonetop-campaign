468  Vor Svetelik  The   Green Lords   (page 210) called this place “the Great White City.” It was a place of beauty, learning, and study. But when the   Fae   (page 94) rebelled, the scholars of Vor Svetelik were tasked with breeding beasts of war. When the Green Lords turned in desperation to the   Things Below   (page 412), the city’s waters fouled and its white trees grew sickly. When other  Makers   (page 264) shattered what is now  Ferrier’s Fen   (page 116), this city became the Green Lords’ last refuge and the site of the war’s last battle. The Green Lords made the invaders pay. They laid traps. They sent their loyal ser- vants to die in waves. They unleashed new horrors, each worse than the last, retreating all the while. And when the Fae and their  Stone Lord   (page 382) allies reached the center of the city, they found it… empty. Naught remained of the Green Lords, except tunnels leading deep into the earth, collapsed behind them. The Stone Lords retreated to their hills. The Fae fell to bickering and their armies disbanded. Those few mortal servants of the Green Lords who survived were left to fend for themselves, eventually becoming the   Forest Folk   (page 150). And Vor Svetelik, the Great White City, was left a blighted, infested ruin.  Impressions  A scraggly canopy in the valley below, hints of streets and buildings, a broken white tower  Pale and spindly trees, bearing withered leaves and oozing black sap  A steady, damp warmth, like foul breath, even in the dead of winter  That feeling you get when you move a rock and see the grubs and worms squirming beneath, but constantly, everywhere you look  Hooks  An artifact that the PCs desperately want lies in the city’s ruins  A southern   antiquarian   (page 260) knows where to find the ruins, and is leading a substantial expedition there, using Stonetop as a basecamp  A Fae chieftain   (page 111) is besieging the ruins, attempting to finally exter- minate the Fomoraij  An earthquake cracks open the ruins; horrors vomit forth into the surround- ing woods

They should be at least a few days’ travel from Stonetop. To find the ruins, PCs will need a guide or detailed directions.  A Fae, a   spirit of the wild   (page 356), or the   Forest Folk   (page 150) could guide them, but wouldn’t want to. All sane beings shun the ruins.  A Green Lord ruin might contain a map or directions, but the PCs would risk getting lost. Such instructions would be centuries out of date!  Magic like the   Sylvan Web   (page 521) could help the PCs find the ruins themselves, though they’d still risk getting lost on the way.  The   West Road   (page 270) once led from where Stonetop stands to Vor Svetelik. It’s broken and long-buried, but could show the way to one who knew what to look for. The undercity , old sewers, cisterns, tunnels, basements, and vaults; linked via erosion, collapse, and beast-burrows.
