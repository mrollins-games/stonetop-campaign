104  This chapter is addressed to players, but GMs should read it, too!  The contents of this chapter are available as PDFs in a downloadable playkit at www.lampblackandbrimstone.com. They are included here for reference, not for use during play.  Playbooks  When you create a PC for   Stonetop , the first thing you’ll do is pick your playbook. Each playbook contains a set of choices, prompts, and options that point towards a specific character archetype, while leaving room to make your character unique. Your playbook also serves as a character sheet. You’ll use it to track your PC’s stats, debilities, current HP and XP, moves, and so forth. The playbooks are designed to be printed double-sided and folded down the middle, so that they form a little booklet. The nine standard playbooks are:  The Blessed   (page 105)  The Fox   (page 109)  The Heavy   (page 113)  The Judge   (page 117)  The Lightbearer   (page 121)  The Marshal   (page 125)  The Ranger   (page 129)  The Seeker   (page 133)  The Would-be Hero   (page 137) For an explanation of each component of a PC playbook, see   Your character   (page 50) in the previous chapter. Each PC also has at least one   insert . The standard inserts are also included in this chapter, starting on page 141. When you have questions about particular moves or other elements of a playbook (how they work, what exactly __ means, what constitutes “a creature of darkness,” etc.), talk it out with the GM and the other players. Consider what’s already been established or implied, and work it out as a group. Don’t be surprised if the GM turns such questions back on you. “Well, what do   you  think constitutes a creature of darkness?” This isn’t a trap or a trick! It’s an invitation for you to   help author the world . Answer with whatever seems obvious or interesting to you.
