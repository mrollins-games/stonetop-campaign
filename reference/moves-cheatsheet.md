# Reference — Moves Cheatsheet

## Basic Moves (all players)
| Move | Trigger | Roll |
|------|---------|------|
| **Clash** | Engage an enemy in melee | +STR |
| **Volley** | Attack at range | +DEX |
| **Defy Danger** | Act despite imminent threat | +varies |
| **Defend** | Stand in defense of something | +CON |
| **Seek Insight** | Study a situation/person | +WIS |
| **Persuade** | Convince an NPC | +CHA |
| **Know Things** | Consult accumulated knowledge | +INT |
| **Aid/Interfere** | Help or hinder another PC | +Bond |

## Homefront Moves
| Move | Trigger | Roll |

| **Make a Plan** | Strategize before action | — |
| **Meet with Disaster** | Steading faces catastrophe | — |
| **Muster** | Rally the village for action | +Population |
| **Pull Together** | Communal labor project | +Fortunes |
| **Trade & Barter** | Exchange goods with neighbors | +Prosperity |

## Seasons Change
| Season | Roller | Roll | Key Effect |
|--------|--------|------|-----------|
| Spring | Most hopeful | +Fortunes | 1 seasonal gain; threat on 7-9 |
| Summer | Most content | +Fortunes | 2/1/0 gains; 1d4-1 Surplus |
| Autumn | Most determined | +Fortunes | 1 gain; threat on 7-9; harvest 1d4 Surplus |
| Winter | Weariest | 1d4+Pop consumed | Survive or lose something precious |

## Results Key
| Roll | Name | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|
| 10+ | Strong hit | Full success, no complications |
| 7–9 | Weak hit | Success with cost, complication, or hard choice |
| 6- | Miss | GM makes a move; mark XP |
