# Steading Improvements

## Status Key
- ⬜ **Known** — Available but not started
- 🟡 **In Progress** — Requirements partially met
- ✅ **Complete** — All requirements met, benefits active
- 🔒 **Locked** — Not yet revealed by the GM

## Known Improvements

### ⬜ Additional Housing
**Summary:** More room to live.  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Exceptional engineer/foreman OR building on fields (-1 Surplus/harvest)
- [ ] Pull Together ×5 (each: 1 season, 1 Surplus, wagonload of timber Value 2)

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; consume 1 less Surplus in winter (Population counts as 1 lower)

---

### ⬜ Aurochs Hunting
**Summary:** Hunt the large herds on the Flats in spring.  
**Requirements (2 of 3):**
- [ ] A Herd of Horses (and hunters to ride them)
- [ ] Cooperating with the Hillfolk
- [ ] A cunning plan
- [ ] A successful first hunt (played out in detail)

**When complete:** Add "Aurochs hunting (meat, hide, horn)" to Resources. Spring hunt roll +Defenses.

---

### ⬜ Expanded Trades
**Summary:** Support more specialized tradesfolk.  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] One of: Harnessing the Stream / Raincatching / Mill
- [ ] At least 3 specialist tradesfolk established (Value 2–3 each)

**When complete:** +1 Prosperity

---

### ⬜ Greater Harvest
**Summary:** Double crop yield or clear new fields.  
**Requirements (1 of):**
- [ ] Double yield inside Old Wall
- [ ] Clear/tame fields beyond Old Wall

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; +1d4 Surplus each autumn

---

### ⬜ Harnessing the Stream
**Summary:** A shallow creek flows below town.  
**Requirements (2 of):**
- [ ] Reservoir + way for water to flow uphill
- [ ] Series of aqueducts from Stream's source

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; add to Resources; 1 Surplus on 7+ spring Fortunes

---

### ⬜ Herd of Horses
**Summary:** A dozen fine steeds.  
**Requirements (all):**
- [ ] Site for stable and corral
- [ ] Pull Together to build (1 month, timber Value 2)
- [ ] Someone skilled in riding/training
- [ ] Acquire ~12 horses
- [ ] Train/break them
- [ ] Additional saddles, harness, plows (Value 2)
- [ ] Pull Together for riding lessons (1 season, 1 Surplus)
- [ ] Full-time keeper

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; replace draft horses with herd; half-time/cost Pull Together; etc.

---

### ⬜ Heroic Reputation
**Summary:** Become known beyond Stonetop.  
**Requirements (any 3):**
- [ ] Impress a band of Hillfolk
- [ ] Brave a lake and return with proof
- [ ] Save many Marshedge residents
- [ ] Save many Gordin's Delve residents
- [ ] Save someone from beyond Marshedge
- [ ] Hire a minstrel (Value 2)

**When complete:** Gain "first meeting" roll +Fortunes move

---

### ⬜ Inn
**Summary:** A proper inn for travelers.  
**Requirements (all, in order):**
- [ ] Designated building site
- [ ] Competent engineer/foreman
- [ ] Furnishings, equipment, material (Value 3)
- [ ] Pull Together ×2 (each: 1 season, 1 Surplus, timber Value 2)
- [ ] Small devoted staff

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; seasonal news roll; 1 Surplus to clear debility

---

### ⬜ Market
**Summary:** Become a trade destination.  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Compelling exclusive good/service OR other reason to visit
- [ ] Dedicated market site
- [ ] Trusted arbiter
- [ ] 4 seasons without notable incidents

**When complete:** +1 Prosperity; generates 1 Surplus spring/summer/autumn if Pop ≥ +1

---

### ⬜ Mill
**Summary:** Better bread, more free time.  
**Requirements (all):**
- [ ] Exceptional engineer/foreman
- [ ] Consistent power source
- [ ] Building site
- [ ] Pull Together ×2 (each: 1 season, 1 Surplus, timber Value 2, rope/supplies Value 2)
- [ ] Full-time miller

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; +1 Surplus each autumn; supplies get +1 use

---

### ⬜ Palisade
**Summary:** A 10' wall of sharpened logs.  
**Requirements (all, in order):**
- [ ] ~20–25 wagonloads timber (Value 3)
- [ ] Competent engineer/foreman
- [ ] Rope, nails, pitch (Value 2)
- [ ] Pull Together (1 month, 1 Surplus)

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; advantage to Deploy behind palisade

---

### ⬜ Raincatching
**Summary:** Cunning roofs, gutters, and conduits.  
**Requirements (all, in order):**
- [ ] Exceptional engineer/foreman
- [ ] Enough slate/terracotta (Value 3)
- [ ] Pull Together ×3 (each: 1 season, 1 Surplus)

**When complete:** +1 Fortunes; 1 Surplus on 7+ summer Fortunes

---

### ⬜ Standing Watch
**Summary:** Full-time warriors.  
**Requirements (all):**
- [ ] Veteran warrior commander
- [ ] At least 6 equipped warriors
- [ ] Village leaders agree to support them

**When complete:** Consumes 1 Surplus/season; treat Defenses as +1 when involving the watch

---

### ⬜ Stone Wall
**Summary:** A mighty rampart of stone.  
**Requirements (all, in order):**
- [ ] Exceptional engineer/foreman
- [ ] Stonecutter with able crew
- [ ] Equipment/tools/material (Value 3)
- [ ] Pull Together ×4 (each: 1 season, 1 Surplus, supplies Value 2)

**When complete:** Advantage to Deploy; consume 1 less Surplus in winter

---

### ⬜ Township
**Summary:** Grow beyond a backwater village.  
**Requirements (all):**
- [ ] Population +3 for 4 consecutive seasons
- [ ] Additional Housing
- [ ] Raincatching OR Harnessing the Stream
- [ ] At least 4 other improvements
- [ ] Formal government

**When complete:** Size → Town, Population resets to +0; advantage on Muster/Pull Together/Trade & Barter

---

### ⬜ Weapons of War
**Summary:** Proper weapons beyond spears.  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Acquire weapons (Value 3) OR smith with staff + iron ore (Value 2) + 4 seasons work
- [ ] Veteran warrior commander
- [ ] Pull Together for training (1 season, 1 Surplus)

**When complete:** +1 Defenses; 1 Surplus/spring to maintain; swords/axes become common items

---

### ⬜ Well-Trained Militia
**Summary:** Drilled fighters with specific tactics.  
**Requirements:**
- [ ] Veteran warrior commander
- [ ] Pull Together per tactic (1 season drills, 1 Surplus each)

**Tactics available:**
- [ ] Archery
- [ ] Cavalry (requires Herd of Horses)
- [ ] Formations
- [ ] Readiness
- [ ] Skirmishing

**When complete:** Position of strength when Deploying with trained tactic; +1 Defenses at 2+ tactics. 1 Surplus + 1 week/summer to maintain or lose 1 tactic.
