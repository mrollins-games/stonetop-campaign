# Blush-of-Dawn

**Type:** Magical Entity (Fae)  
**Status:** 🔴 Not yet introduced  
**Instinct:** To be adored

## Summary

Blush-of-Dawn is a Fae entity bred to be loved. They were the shining jewel of a Green Lord's paradise — the beauty of a rising sun made flesh. When their master's tomb was plundered during the Fae rebellion, they were left bereft, vulnerable, alone and unloved. They have no interest in freedom — only the safe warm surety of other beings' full regard.

Their domain is a stately pavilion in a summer glade, forever in that moment just before dawn. Filled with fawning victims of their charms: lovers, would-be lovers, abducted children, devotees. On the outskirts: cast-out wretches who've lost favor but whose jealous adoration still feeds the Blush-of-Dawn.

## Stats (from source)

**Solitary, fae, magical, beautiful, passionate**  
HP 12; Armor 4 (resilience), 0 vs. iron  
Damage: fiery touch d8 w/disadvantage (hand)

## Monster Moves

- Sing a song to stir the blood
- Inspire passion / jealousy / devotion
- Steal away into their Fae domain, perhaps with a new prize in tow
- Set devotees to a task / challenge
- Blaze brightly, blind onlookers

## The Leanan Sidhe Pattern

Blush-of-Dawn follows the folklore pattern of the **Leanan Sidhe** (fairy muse-lover): they offer genuine, intoxicating gifts — brilliance, clarity, purpose — in exchange for devotion. The gifts are REAL. The work produced is extraordinary. But the devotee wastes away, growing pale, obsessive, thin, consumed. They die adored.

### What Blush-of-Dawn Might Offer Each PC

| PC | Offer | The Pattern |
|---|---|---|

| **Gareth** (Seeker) | Answers. Every mystery resolves. He knows where things are, what happened, why — without searching | He becomes the smartest person in any room. But his curiosity dies — replaced by certainty. He grows listless between revelations, craving the next fix |
| **Wolfram** (Blessed) | His patron's voice becomes constant, intimate, warm — the loneliness of devotion replaced with perfect communion | He becomes serene, unshakable, radiantly faithful. But he stops needing other people entirely. The village loses him before his body fails |
| **Ted** (Judge) | Every ruling he makes is obviously correct. No one argues. No ambiguity. Perfect law from his mouth | He becomes the law made flesh. But justice without struggle becomes tyranny — people obey from awe, not respect. He becomes brittle and gaunt |
| **Filberta** (Would-Be Hero) | Deeds that actually work. Every swing lands. Every leap sticks. She becomes the hero she imagines | She becomes legendary overnight. But the gap between her real self and the gifted self widens — she can't tell which accomplishments are hers anymore |

## Stonetop Connection

The Stream hooks (Book II) suggest: *"Haunting songs are heard on misty days; hot-blooded youths are intent on finding the source (an entrancing Fae, like Blush-of-Dawn)."*

This means Blush-of-Dawn could manifest near the Stream — drawing Stonetopians into the woods with song before the PCs ever see the pavilion.

## Grim Portents

1. [ ] Townsfolk hear impossibly beautiful music at dawn and can't place the source
2. [ ] A young person (NPC) starts producing astonishing work — art, song, craft — while visibly wasting
3. [ ] More people wander toward the Stream at twilight; some don't come back until morning, dazed and happy
4. [ ] A circle of devotees forms in the village — they defend the music, resent anyone who questions it
5. [ ] **Impending Doom:** Blush-of-Dawn has enough devotees to sustain a permanent court. The taken don't return.

## Stakes

- Can devotion be freely given AND harmful? Where's the line between love and parasitism?
- Will the PCs respect villagers' choice to stay, or intervene against their will?
- What does Blush-of-Dawn actually FEEL? Are they a predator or a lonely, broken thing?
- Is there a way to satisfy their need without sacrifice?

## GM Moves

- Sing a song that reaches exactly the person most vulnerable right now
- Make a devotee produce something undeniably brilliant (force the PCs to confront that the gift is REAL)
- Have a cast-out wretch beg for readmission — show what losing favor looks like
- Blaze radiantly when challenged — force the confronter to look away or be moved
- Set two devotees against each other in jealous competition for favor

## The Domain (Fae POI)

A stately pavilion in a glade frozen in the moment before dawn. The sky is perpetually rose-gold. The air smells of honeysuckle and warmth. Everything is beautiful. Everyone inside is adoring, creating, wasting.

**Outer ring:** The cast-outs. Wretched, jealous, begging for a second audience. They'll do ANYTHING to get back in.

**Inner ring:** Active devotees. Painting, singing, crafting, gazing. Thin. Bright-eyed. Happy. Dying.

**Center:** Blush-of-Dawn, resplendent, surrounded by regard.
