# Lord Wokn

**Type:** Fae / Political  
**Status:** 🟡 Dormant  
**Instinct:** To be known — and through knowing, to bind

## Summary

Lord Wokn is a Fae lord whose power operates through *knowledge itself*. His encampment manifests wherever he becomes known. To make this so, various Fae agents spread awareness of him through tricks of exchange — each interaction plants the seed of knowing. Once enough people know of Wokn, he arrives. Once he arrives, he makes demands.

His demand: Stonetop's people must take up the purification rites at the cistern (C9). You are Forest Folk now. The Wood demands it.

## The Mechanism of Knowing

Wokn's presence spreads through a chain of Fae agents, each offering knowledge in exchange for... knowing it.

### The Dealer

> "I have a special deal for you. Do you want to know it?"

- **If yes:** He tells you something. Then, slowly: "In exchange... *you will know it.*"
- **If no:** "Alright. Well — now you know of a knowing, but don't yet know it."

Either way, a seed is planted.

### The Girl

> "Will you know something from me?"

A simple offer. Accepting means carrying the knowledge. The cost is invisible.

### The Trickster

Offers a book. The book describes an encampment of the Fae. Reading it is knowing it. Knowing it makes it real.

### Near the Malachite Formations

A Fae offers to trade information. The cost is that you know it.

## The Encampment

**Trigger:** "Wokn is known — to return to — these parts."

Once enough knowing accumulates, the PCs stumble upon a large tent encampment. The head tent is massive. Behind it, dozens more tents — the camp seems far larger than it should be.

### The Grand Hall

The inside of the head tent is impossibly large — a grand hall. Revelers line both sides of a long welcome carpet. They are doing various things they enjoy — feasting, dancing, gambling, sculpting, arguing philosophy, sleeping in piles, dueling with wooden swords, painting, laughing at nothing.

As the PCs walk down the hall, revelers offer them to partake. Each PC is offered whatever they revel in most — the Fae know, somehow. They always know.

### Lord Wokn

At the far end of the hall, a raised platform with a large comfortable bed-couch. Sat upon it — not directly facing them, slightly askew, as if they arrived mid-thought — is Lord Wokn.

This is the first time the PCs have met a lord. What even IS that? What does lordship mean among the Fae?

He welcomes them. He offers offerings. He is generous and warm and completely in control.

Then he tells them:

> "The Wood demands humans take up the cistern purification rites. You are Forest Folk now."

A summary of the demand: the purification cistern at C9 once maintained by the Forest Folk must be tended again. The ley line weakens. The corruption spreads. Someone must do the work. Wokn has decided it will be Stonetop.

## The Adornments

Wokn's proxies offer these as gifts to decorate the town — beautiful, welcome, subtly binding.

### Carved Lintel-Stones
Smooth malachite-green stones etched with "protective" knotwork, offered for above doorframes. The symbols are actually Fae script. Every resident who passes beneath reads them unconsciously — each passage deepens the knowing without anyone realizing they're learning something.

### A Gilded Weather-Vane
For the granary roof. Elegant metalwork — a running hare in profile. Always points east regardless of wind direction. People stop noticing after a week. Those who DO notice feel a pull to look where it points.

### Tallow Candles That Smell of the Forest
A generous gift — dozens of candles, offered freely. When lit, they smell of deep wood: moss, hazel, loam. The shadows they cast don't quite match the objects they belong to. Shadows lean east. Shadows reach toward doorways. Seeds the shadow-breaking grim portent — the candles are practice, teaching shadows to move independently.

## Portent Triggers (Escalation)

Things that advance Wokn's demand toward consequence:

- [ ] Someone accepts what is offered (at the encampment or from an agent)
- [ ] Someone brings a Fae offering back to Stonetop
- [ ] A PC speaks Wokn's name in Stonetop (spreads the knowing)
- [ ] The party refuses the demand and leaves

---

## Grim Portents

1. [ ] Stonetopians' shadows break free of them and try to lead them toward the purification cistern (C9) — shadows pulling east, detaching partially, pointing
2. [ ] Fae appear at the far edge of the river, calling townsfolk over — friendly, inviting, beckoning from the E5 bank
3. [ ] The knowing spreads through Stonetop — people dream of the encampment without having been there
4. [ ] **Impending Doom:** The Fae consider the demand refused. Consequences unknown — but Wokn is patient, and patience in a Fae lord is terrifying

## Stakes

- What happens if the PCs accept the demand? Is tending the cistern even possible?
- Can the knowing be un-known? Can you forget Wokn?
- What does "You are Forest Folk now" actually mean? Is it a title? A curse? A biological change?
- Is Wokn connected to the original contract, or is this something new?
- What do the Malachite formation inscriptions say about Wokn (if anything)?
- Does Filberta's fairy friend know Wokn? Is the friend one of his agents?

---

## GM Moves

- Have a Fae agent appear and offer knowledge at an inconvenient moment
- Show a PC's shadow behaving independently (pulling east)
- Describe townsfolk mentioning dreams of a tent, a carpet, a lord they've never met
- Have the encampment appear somewhere it wasn't before
- Wokn sends a gift — generous, beautiful, with no stated strings
