## Threat Types Reference
From the Stonetop rules:

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Villain** | An NPC with evil/selfish intent and the power to act on it |
| **Rabble** | A group of hostile creatures (crinwin, bandits, etc.) |
| **Wildcard** | An NPC who causes trouble unintentionally or unpredictably |
| **Magical Entity** | A supernatural being (spirit, fae, bound entity) |
| **Supernatural** | A curse, plague, magical phenomenon |
| **Environmental** | Weather, terrain, natural disaster |
| **Political** | Faction conflict, power struggle, social pressure |
