# Skill: /know-things

> Generate answers for when a PC rolls Know Things about a topic.
> Provides tiered information based on roll result.

## When to Use
- A PC triggers Know Things
- User asks "what would [PC] know about [X]?"

## Procedure

### Step 1: Identify Topic & PC
- What are they asking about?
- Which PC is rolling? (their background/origin might give bonus knowledge)
- What's the roll result? (10+ / 7-9 / 6-)

### Step 2: Research the Topic
1. Find the relevant Book II entry via `reference/indexes/lore-index.md`
2. Read the entry, noting what's marked as "common knowledge" vs. "lore" vs. "secrets"
3. Check if the PC's playbook/background gives special insight

### Step 3: Generate Tiered Answers

```markdown
## Know Things: [Topic]

### On a 10+ (tell them all of this)
- [Fact 1 — common knowledge]
- [Fact 2 — useful detail]
- [Fact 3 — specific actionable info]
- Plus: ask "what else do you want to know?" and answer truthfully

### On a 7-9 (tell them this)
- [Fact 1 — common knowledge]
- [Fact 2 — partial/vague]
- GM picks: the info is incomplete, or it comes with a complication

### On a 6- (GM move)
- They still might know something basic (or not)
- GM makes a move: reveal unwelcome truth, put them in a spot, etc.
- Suggested unwelcome truth: [something true but alarming from the entry]
```

### Step 4: Consider PC-Specific Knowledge
- **The Seeker** — knows ancient/magical/historical things
- **The Blessed** — knows nature, spirits, old ways
- **The Judge** — knows law, politics, settlements
- **The Ranger** — knows terrain, creatures, survival
- **The Fox** — knows people, rumors, trade routes
- **The Marshal** — knows warfare, tactics, threats
- **The Lightbearer** — knows religion, undead, darkness
