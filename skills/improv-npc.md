# Skill: /improv-npc

> Quick NPC improvisation during live play — generate voice, motivation, and knowledge
> for an NPC the PCs are talking to right now.

## When to Use
- Mid-session, PCs approach an NPC and GM needs to roleplay them
- User asks "what would [NPC] say about...?" / "how does [NPC] react?"

## Procedure

### Step 1: Look Up the NPC
Check `npcs/_npc-index.md`:
- Do we have a file for them? Read it.
- What are their traits? Occupation? Relations?
- What sessions have they appeared in? (Check for established behavior)

### Step 2: Determine What They Know
Based on their occupation, location, and traits:
- A **publican** knows gossip, travelers' tales, who's drinking too much
- A **smith** knows about tools, trade goods, Gordin's Delve
- A **farmer** knows weather, land, neighbors' business
- A **hunter** knows the Wood, animal patterns, dangers outside town
- A **trader** knows prices, road conditions, news from Marshedge

Cross-reference with:
- Active threats they might have noticed
- Recent session events that would affect them
- Their traits (e.g., "knows all the gossip" = they know everything)

### Step 3: Generate Response

```markdown
## [NPC Name] responds:

**Demeanor:** [based on traits — e.g., "cautious and suspicious" or "eager to help"]
**Voice note:** [a quick characterization hint — e.g., "speaks slowly, avoids eye contact"]

**What they say (in essence):**
[Summary of what they'd communicate]

**What they hold back:**
[Things they know but won't volunteer — requires Persuade or building trust]

**What they want:**
[Their current concern/desire that might color the conversation]
```

### Step 4: Update Records
If anything new is established about this NPC during the interaction:
- Note it for the session file
- Suggest updating their NPC file after the session
