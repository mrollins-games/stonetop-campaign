# Skill: /season-change

> Walk the GM through the Seasons Change procedure step by step.

## When to Use
- Time to transition between seasons in-game
- User asks "how do I run Seasons Change?" or "it's time for winter"

## Procedure

### Step 1: Identify the Season
Which season are we entering? The procedure differs:

| Season | Roller | Stat | Key Effects |
|--------|--------|------|-------------|
| Spring | Most hopeful PC | +Fortunes | 1 gain; threat on 7-9; threats abound on 6- |
| Summer | Most content PC | +Fortunes | 2/1/0 gains; generate 1d4-1 Surplus |
| Autumn | Most determined PC | +Fortunes | 1 gain; threat on 7-9; harvest 1d4 Surplus |
| Winter | Weariest PC | special | Consume 1d4+Pop Surplus; survive or lose |

### Step 2: Pre-Roll Checklist
Before rolling:
- [ ] Update `steading/stonetop.md` with any changes from last season
- [ ] Note current Fortunes value
- [ ] Identify who rolls (which PC fits the descriptor?)
- [ ] Review active threats (any about to advance?)

### Step 3: Run the Roll
Guide the GM through:
1. Who rolls and what they roll
2. Interpret the result (read exact move text from source)
3. If seasonal gains: present options
4. If threat introduced/worsened: invoke `/create-threat` or advance existing

### Step 4: Post-Roll Updates
- Update `steading/stonetop.md` (Surplus, Fortunes reset to +1)
- Update `world/timeline.md` (log the roll and result)
- If threat advanced: update `threats/_threat-tracker.md`
- Check if any improvements trigger ("when spring breaks forth...", "when the harvest is complete...")
- Check `steading/improvements.md` for seasonal effects

### Step 5: Between-Season Flavor
Suggest 2–3 vignettes or questions to make the season feel real:
- What's the weather like?
- What are the villagers doing?
- How do the PCs spend this time?
