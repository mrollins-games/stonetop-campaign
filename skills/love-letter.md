# Skill: /love-letter

> Write a "love letter" — a custom between-session move addressed to a specific PC.
> Used to resolve downtime, advance off-screen action, or kick off a session with a bang.

## When to Use
- Between sessions when a PC was doing something off-screen
- To open a session with immediate momentum
- When time passes and something should happen to a specific PC

## Procedure

### Step 1: Context
- Which PC?
- What were they doing (or what happened to them)?
- What's the dramatic question?

### Step 2: Read the Rules
Check `reference/source-text/book-i-chapters/16-writing-moves.md` for love letter format and examples.

Key principles:
- Address the character directly ("Dear [Name],...")
- Describe the situation
- Give them a roll
- 10+ = good outcome + bonus
- 7-9 = mixed outcome / hard choice
- 6- = things went badly (GM makes a move)

### Step 3: Generate the Love Letter

```markdown
## Love Letter to [Character Name]

Dear [Name],

[1–2 paragraphs setting the scene — what happened during the time skip, what you were trying to do]

**Roll +[STAT]:**

**On a 10+**, choose 2:
- [Good outcome 1]
- [Good outcome 2]
- [Good outcome 3]

**On a 7-9**, choose 1 from above, but also:
- [Complication or cost]

**On a 6-**, [bad outcome — GM makes a move]

Whatever happens, [thing that's true regardless of the roll].

Love and [thematic word],
The GM
```

### Step 4: Connect to Campaign
- Tie outcomes to active threats, NPCs, or pending plot threads
- Make sure results are recordable (can update campaign files after)
