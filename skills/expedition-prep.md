# Skill: /expedition-prep

> Help the GM prepare for an expedition — the PCs are leaving town.

## When to Use
- Players declare they're heading somewhere
- User asks "help me prep the journey to [X]"

## Procedure

### Step 1: Identify Destination & Route
- Where are they going?
- How are they getting there? (road, wilderness, river, etc.)
- What season is it? (affects weather, hazards, travel time)

### Step 2: Research the Route
Check relevant Book II entries along the path:
- Starting point entry
- Terrain entries (Flats, Great Wood, Foothills, etc.)
- Destination entry
- `reference/indexes/lore-index.md` for all relevant entries

### Step 3: Identify Challenges
From the source material and current threats, build a journey:

```markdown
## Expedition: [Origin] → [Destination]

### Route Overview
- **Distance/Time:** [estimate from source]
- **Season:** [current] — [weather implications]
- **Terrain:** [types traversed]

### Challenges (present in order during Chart a Course)
1. **[Challenge]** — [what it involves, which moves it triggers]
2. **[Challenge]** — [...]
3. **[Surprise]** — [something they couldn't have predicted]

### Dangers Along the Way
[From monster-index.md and Book II entries]
| Creature | Where | HP | Likelihood |
|----------|-------|----|-----------|

### Discoveries Possible
[Interesting things they could find]
- [Discovery 1]
- [Discovery 2]

### At the Destination
[What they'll find when they arrive]
- [Key NPCs present]
- [Current state of the location]
- [Active threats here]
```

### Step 4: Connect to Active Threats
- Do any active threats have grim portents visible along this route?
- Could a threat's minions be encountered?

### Step 5: Prep the Site (if destination is an adventure site)
Reference `reference/source-text/book-i-chapters/11-sites.md` for site design guidance.
