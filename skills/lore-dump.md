# Skill: /lore-dump

> Provide a comprehensive briefing on a setting topic — place, creature, faction, god, etc.

## When to Use
- User asks "tell me about [topic]" / "what do I know about [X]?"
- Before introducing a location or faction in play
- When players ask an NPC about something and GM needs to improvise knowledgeably

## Procedure

### Step 1: Find the Source
1. Check `reference/indexes/lore-index.md` for the topic
2. Open the full Book II entry file
3. Read it completely (Book II entries average 19KB — manageable)

### Step 2: Check Campaign Context
- Has this topic come up in play before? Check session files
- Are there active threats connected to it? Check `threats/_threat-tracker.md`
- Are there NPCs connected? Check `npcs/_npc-index.md`
- Any GM ideas related? Check `gm-notes/ideas.md`

### Step 3: Present Briefing

```markdown
## [Topic] — Lore Briefing

### The Basics (what PCs would commonly know)
[Summary appropriate for a Know Things roll on a 10+]

### GM-Only Details
[Things only the GM knows — secrets, hidden truths, connections]

### Hooks & Adventure Seeds
[Potential ways this could become relevant to play]
- [Hook 1]
- [Hook 2]

### Dangers Present
[Monsters, hazards, or threats associated with this topic]
| Creature | HP | Damage | Source |
|----------|----|----|--------|

### Connections to Your Campaign
[How this relates to existing threats, NPCs, or events in YOUR game]

### Questions to Ask Players
[If introducing this topic, what questions could seed player ownership?]
```

### Step 4: Offer Follow-Ups
- "Want me to create a location file for this?"
- "Should I prep a threat based on what's here?"
- "Want a Know Things table for this topic?"
