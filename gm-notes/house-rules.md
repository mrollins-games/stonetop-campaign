# House Rules

> Modifications, clarifications, or additions to the Stonetop rules for this campaign.
> Copilot should reference these when answering rules questions — house rules override RAW.

## Active House Rules

### 1. Duplicate Playbooks Allowed
**Rule:** Multiple players may choose the same playbook. Each character must still be unique in background, origin, and personality.  
**Reason:** Player fun > mechanical restriction. With 5 PCs, limiting to unique playbooks constrains choice too much.  
**Replaces/Modifies:** RAW implies one playbook per player (only one copy printed per class).  
**Added:** 2026-05-28  
**Session Introduced:** Pre-Session Zero

---

### 2. Extra Judge (Absent Player XP)
**Rule:** When David (Ted, the Judge) misses a session, he picks one of the following conditions. He gets 6 circles to mark; each time the condition is met in the next session he attends, mark a circle. When all 6 are filled, mark XP.  
**Conditions (pick 1):**
- Cite a law exactly as written
- Get someone to say the word "Judge"
- Make the Intervene move when someone breaks a law
- Hold court with 2+ people present

**Reason:** Keeps Ted engaged with his playbook identity even when David can't attend. Reward is modest (6 triggers → 1 XP) so it doesn't snowball.  
**Replaces/Modifies:** No RAW equivalent — custom addition.  
**Added:** 2026-06-12  
**Session Introduced:** TBD
