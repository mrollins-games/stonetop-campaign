### Major Arcana: The Azure Hand
- Thick staff of gray metal topped with a plate of etherium in the shape of a stylized hand
- Palm embossed with cloud and lightning bolts
- Smells of ozone; spine never ceases to tingle in its presence
- **Powers:** Sense energy currents; gather energy in a swirling vortex; can direct into a vessel, discharge as damage, or fuel other magic
- **Risk:** Corruption/overcharge — on 6-, mark a box. When 4 boxes filled, power goes out of control.
- Gareth is cautious with it, studying it more than using it

### Minor Arcana
1. **Scroll Case** (MASTERED) — Leather scroll case, contains 2 bluish etherium ingots. Grants access to "Lauge Davis Galvanic Infusion" (absorb power from etherium).
2. **The Key** (UNMASTERED) — A gleaming white key-like thing. Hurts to look at. Makes you aware of your own dying flesh. Must master fear and hold it tight, endure weeks of migraines/flashes of color.
3. **Fay-wood Broom** (LEAD ONLY — not yet found) — A broom made of Fay wood with magical properties. Located somewhere near Stonetop.
