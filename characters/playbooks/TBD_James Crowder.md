## Smith/Apprentice Connection

The book (Getting Started p.27) specifically asks players with a Smithy:
- Are you the town's smith, or their apprentice, or what?
- How long have you been doing this?
- How'd you get started?

This will be asked during Turn 2 (Special Possessions) regardless of playbook choice.

### If Heavy:
**Turn 1 (Background):**
