## Background: Missionary

You are part of a larger order of Judges, sent here to protect the flickering flame of civilization. The Chronicle is relatively new; your position in town is far from certain.

**Fellow Judges** (pick 2 more to add to Neighbors):
- ☐ Devin (from Marshedge)
- ☐ Haeris (from Gordin's Delve)
- ☐ Isalde (from the Manmarch)
- ☐ Rahat (from Lygos)
- ☐ Tejisha (from Barrier Pass)
- ☐ Unz (from the Hillfolk)

**Ability:** When you call upon the Judge of another steading for aid or information, they are oathbound to give it. You are likewise oathbound to support them.

## Moves

**Starting moves:** Censure, Chronicler of Stonetop, + 2 more of your choice

### Censure
When you first denounce an individual in your presence as an agent of chaos or anathema to civilization, they pick 1: ashamed, doubtful, afraid, or enraged.

### Chronicler of Stonetop
When you write up detailed session notes and share them with the other players, hold +1 Diligence. Spend 1 Diligence to add +1 to a roll you or a fellow player just made.
