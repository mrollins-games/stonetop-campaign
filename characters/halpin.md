## Notes
- Solar-gazed as a child (age 4-5), went blind, saw a vision of Helior who "caressed his face and told him truths" — this is when he gained his powers
- Makes mead from honey; brandy/whisky is "Helior's trapped sunlight" — saved for winter warmth
- Meditates during peak sun hours (avoids hard field labor during this time)
- House is the social hub at night — well-lit, open door, people come to talk
- Never disparages anyone; every mistake is a learning opportunity
- "Every sunrise is a new day and a new chance to do good"
- People are friendly to him because he genuinely makes them feel good (Radiant)
