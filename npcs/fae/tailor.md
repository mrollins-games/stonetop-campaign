# The Tailor

**Location:** The Tailor's Shop, Faeton  
**Type:** Fae NPC  
**Role:** Measurement collector / body-alteration dealer

## Description

A fastidious Fae with endless measuring tape, precise manners, and an obsessive interest in dimensions. Neat, particular, and utterly focused on numbers.

## Behavior

1. **Measures PCs immediately** on entry — height, arm span, stride, everything. Doesn't ask permission, just does it cheerfully and matter-of-factly.

2. **Asks for "feats"** — precise measurements of things they've encountered:
   - How tall a building is
   - The width of a river
   - The depth of a well
   - The distance between two landmarks
   - Any specific, verifiable measurement

3. **Trades:** If given a measurement he hasn't collected, he offers his **calling card**:
   > "Call if you wish to reconsider your measurements."

## The Calling Card

When invoked, the bearer may **temporarily alter one of their own physical measurements**:
- Height
- Reach
- Weight
- Stride

Duration and limitations TBD — likely one scene or until the next rest.

## GM Notes
- Rewards players who pay attention to the world and take notes
- Non-threatening, quirky — a "service" encounter rather than a challenge
- The card is a useful tool that encourages exploration and observation
- Could become a recurring relationship if PCs bring him measurements from their travels
- Never threatens, never bargains hard — he just *wants numbers*
