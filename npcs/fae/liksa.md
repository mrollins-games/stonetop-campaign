# Liksa

**Pronouns:** she/her  
**Home:** Fae realm (has a domain accessible beneath her dark stone patio)  
**Nature:** Fae — snake-bodied entity, textile sovereign  
**Traits:** Vain, industrious, commanding, deeply self-conscious  
**Desires:** To be seen as beautiful; to collect items that make her so; to never have her snake-form seen

## Description

Liksa emerges from the center of her domain — rising from piled bedding as if on a stage elevator, simultaneously donning a robe from the pile. She presents as beautiful and composed. Her true form is hidden: below the robes she is serpentine.

**True Form (hidden, beneath her domain):** Her body is ~10 beautiful nude human torsos stitched together one atop another; her tail/head is a snake. A reflection of above — her domain inverts here.

## Domain — The Textile Court

A concentric, circular compound organized around production of cloth and fiber. Everything moves with frantic, purposeful energy.

### Outer Areas (Emanating from center)
- Small but hurriedly-worked and *impossibly productive* plots of cotton, flax, and hemp arranged in circular patterns

### Inner Areas

**Spider Silk Station:**
- Direct wooden dowels arranged in rows
- Child-sized sheep-faced spiders work frantically, spinning silk and reeling it directly as they traverse each dowel in a spiral

**Moth-Women Weavers:**
- Moth women arched over looms, weaving
- Silk fed to them by young chubby humanoids whose forelimbs resemble bulging, bulbous larvae
- These "silkwormian intermediaries" conduct an abbreviated process — harvesting, spinning, dyeing, and weaving all at once from large cocoons (1⅓ human-sized)

**Alpacian Shearing Salon:**
- Teenage Alpacian boys shear *themselves* into buckets
- They carry their bucket to an attendant at a small wooden stand, trade it for another pair of shears, and return to their salon chair
- Wool picked up by the lengthy arms of teenage girls who sit backless, facing outward at a circular workstation
- Their hair braided into a shared singular strand
- Each one performing a different task: sewing, dyeing, combing, cleaning the wool

### Center — The Dark Patio

- A dark stone raised patio; polished and reflective
- **Mirror property:** With the sun to your back, you see your reflection very clearly in it — *instead of your shadow*
- On top: an outdoor conversation pit filled with colorful cushions, blankets, pillows, and robes
- **Unstable footing:** Walking on it is difficult — your feet partially sink in and the ground is unstable

### Below the Patio — Liksa's True Domain

- If a person gets dragged or dropped below the patio surface, they fall into Liksa's personal domain
- This is the *reflection* of above — inverted
- Here her true serpentine form resides

## Drives & Instincts

| Drive | Expression |
|-------|-----------|
| Be seen as beautiful | Collects fine textiles, robes, adornments; stages her appearances |
| Collect items of beauty | Bargains, trades, possibly coerces for materials and crafted goods |
| Hide her snake-form | Never allows anyone below; reacts with fury/panic if exposed |

## Interaction Notes

- Likely a powerful potential **patron or bargainer** — her domain produces extraordinary textiles
- Her vulnerability (vanity + secret form) is a clear lever for PCs
- Could be encountered via Fae boundary crossing or deep-wood exploration
- The dark patio is both a trap and a threshold
