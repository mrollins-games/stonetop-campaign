# Jonas

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop  
**Occupation:** Town Elder  
**Traits:** Gullible, gifted storyteller, easily swayed  
**Relations:** Senior elder

## Description
One of Stonetop's town elders — nominally the "wisest" but that doesn't make him actually wise. The last person to speak to him tends to be the one whose opinion he adopts. A gifted storyteller, which is likely how he rose to his position.

## Key Facts
- Town elder — people look to him for decisions
- Extremely susceptible to influence — last person he talks to sways him
- Gifted storyteller — can captivate an audience at campfires
- Not actually wise despite his position
- How he got into this position is "not pretty" (vague reference)

## Story Involvement
A political lever. Anyone who wants to influence village decisions just needs to be the last person to talk to Jonas. This makes him both useful and dangerous.
