# Efraim

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop (edge of town)  
**Occupation:** Stargazer / Astrologer  
**Traits:** Social anxiety, nocturnal, awkward  
**Relations:** None close (by choice)

## Description
Stonetop's astronomer and astrologer. Works at night charting the stars, sleeps during the day. Has severe social anxiety — never comes to campfires or gatherings. People respect him and he plays an important community role (advising farmers on planting seasons, tracking weather patterns), but Halpin wishes he'd come to a party just once.

## Key Facts
- Charts the stars, records celestial movements
- Advises farmers on what to plant and when (like a farmers' almanac)
- Works at night, sleeps during the day — can't work in fields
- Never attends social gatherings
- People respect his knowledge but he's isolated
- Has drawings and star charts
- Halpin (and others) want to integrate him socially
