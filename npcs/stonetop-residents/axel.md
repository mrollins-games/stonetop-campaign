# Axel

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop  
**Occupation:** Apprentice cooper (Ted's apprentice)  
**Traits:** Enthusiastic, terrible ideas  
**Relations:** Works for Ted

## Description
Ted's apprentice in barrel-making. Full of enthusiasm and terrible ideas. Always suggesting impractical innovations. The source of the stolen Fay wood that broke the contract with the Great Wood.

## Key Facts
- Makes barrels under Ted's guidance
- All his ideas are terrible and impractical — "Hey, why don't we do this?" / "Can you just make the barrel the way I taught you?"
- Ted put pressure on him to source special wood for barrels
- Axel took Fay wood from the Great Wood — breaking the ancient contract
- Ted didn't explicitly tell him to steal the wood, but indirectly caused it
- Doesn't seem to understand the gravity of what he's done

## Story Involvement
**Critical:** Axel is the unwitting instrument of the broken Fay contract. His theft of Fay wood is the inciting incident for what may become a major threat. He doesn't know what he's done wrong.
