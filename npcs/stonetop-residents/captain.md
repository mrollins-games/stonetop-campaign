# Captain

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop  
**Occupation:** Hunter (hunting party member)  
**Traits:** —  
**Relations:** Member of a hunting party

## Description
A hunter who was part of one of the parties out during the spring storm. Returned to Stonetop alone, wounded (axe wound), comatose, and possessed by a freed undead spirit.

## Key Facts
- Staggered back to the village with a severe axe wound
- Was possessed — a spirit spoke through him in spirit-tongue
- Halpin stabilized his wound with Invoke the Sun God (10+)
- The spirit left him after the healing / interaction at the Birds & Bees
- Currently comatose but stable
- Had a map on his body marking where he was attacked (the logging site)
- Spirit markings on his body match those on trees at the logging site
