# Fflur

**Pronouns:** she/her  
**Home:** Stonetop  
**Occupation:** Gatherer / Town Elder  
**Traits:** Very old (70-80s), sweet, gathers flowers and herbs daily  
**Relations:** Wolfram's charge (neighbor across town square)

## Description
One of Stonetop's elders. A very old woman (late 70s to early 80s) who goes out every day with a little basket and comes back with flowers and herbs. Wolfram watches over her — making sure her candles are out and shutters closed each night.

## Key Facts
- Daily routine: goes out with basket, gathers flowers and herbs, comes home
- Very old — a "town great-grandma" figure
- Wolfram ensures her safety nightly (candles extinguished, shutters shut)
- Lives across the town square from Wolfram — close enough to see her house
- A fire risk if left unattended (candles + old age)
- One of the town elders
