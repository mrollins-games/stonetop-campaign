# Colyn

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop  
**Occupation:** Porter/courier (inter-town trader and messenger)  
**Traits:** Dallies with Fae, well-traveled  
**Relations:** Wolfram's father

## Description
Stonetop's primary connection to other settlements. Leaves town for long periods to trade goods and carry messages between Stonetop, Marshedge, Gordin's Delve, and beyond. In his downtime, he frequently ventures into the Old Woods.

## Key Facts
- Found Wolfram as a baby in the Old Woods and brought him home
- Rumors in town that he has a relationship with dryads or other Fae
- Primary trade connection — takes Stonetop's surplus out and brings back needed goods
- Nobody knows who Wolfram's mother is
