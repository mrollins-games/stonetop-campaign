# Dilwen

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop (spends much time in the forest)  
**Occupation:** Trapper  
**Traits:** Has a lot of backbone, rigid, set in his ways  
**Relations:** Filberta's father, Offda's partner, Tuath's father, Gethin's brother(?)

## Description
A stubborn, rugged man who lives partly in the forest. Wears suspenders, always has a chew in. Traditional and inflexible.

## Key Facts
- Works as a trapper — goes into the woods for extended periods
- Rigid and set in his ways — this is why Filberta doesn't get along with him
- Did not approve of Filberta's relationship with nature/the forest
- Has "a lot of backbone" — not easily swayed
