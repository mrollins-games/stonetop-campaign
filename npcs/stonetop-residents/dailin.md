# Dailin

**Pronouns:** she/her  
**Home:** Stonetop  
**Occupation:** Keeper of Helior's shrine  
**Traits:** Conceited, tedious  
**Relations:** Halpin's mother

## Description
An older woman, well-worn and sun-spotted from a lifetime of sun exposure (following her son's example). She tends Helior's shrine in the Pavilion of the Gods and expects special treatment from everyone due to her son's status as the Chosen of the Sun.

## Key Facts
- Took up sun-bathing after her son became the Blessed of Helior — now has extensive sun damage/spots
- Repeats the same stories about Halpin endlessly ("I delivered half the people in this town" energy)
- Not spiteful, but genuinely believes her position deserves deference
- Gets indignant if asked to do anything else — "I'm so old, my son is the Sun Keeper!"
- Shows up at Halpin's house expecting special treatment from visitors

## Relationships
| With | Nature |
|------|--------|
| Halpin | Mother. Proud of him to the point of obnoxiousness. |
| Gareth | He doesn't put up with her behavior |
| Villagers | Mixed — some dismiss her, some tolerate her |

## Story Involvement
Comic relief and social friction. Represents the gap between Halpin's genuine faith and how religion can become performative/self-serving.
