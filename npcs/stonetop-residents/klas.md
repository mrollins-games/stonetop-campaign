# Klas

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop (originally from Marshedge)  
**Occupation:** Publican (Birds & Bees tavern)  
**Traits:** Thick accent (unintelligible to most), has a beef with Marshedge  
**Relations:** Recruited by Ted

## Description
The publican of the Birds & Bees, Stonetop's public house. Originally from Marshedge but was run out — possibly because people took offense at things he said that they couldn't understand. Has such a thick accent that almost no one in Stonetop can understand him. Only Gareth can communicate with him (via Polyglot skill).

## Key Facts
- Runs the Birds & Bees public house
- Accent is so thick that critical information gets lost in translation
- Has been known to tell important information that nobody understands until days later
- Has a beef with Marshedge (his hometown)
- Ted brought him in via his contacts with Marshedge
- May actually know a great deal about both towns, if anyone could understand him
