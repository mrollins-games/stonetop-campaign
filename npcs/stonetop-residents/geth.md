# Geth

**Pronouns:** he/him  
**Home:** Stonetop  
**Occupation:** Day laborer (unskilled)  
**Traits:** Sweet, kind, very strong, not bright, gentle, baby-faced  
**Relations:** Wolfram admires his generosity

## Description
A massive young man (17-19 years old), well over 6 feet tall, overweight but incredibly strong. Has a baby face and is one of the sweetest, kindest people in the village. Not intellectually gifted — they don't put him in the fields because "you gotta kind of know what you're doing." He carries barrels, moves rocks, does heavy lifting. Would give the shirt off his back to anyone.

## Key Facts
- Very young (17-19), huge, strong, gentle
- Unskilled but incredibly hardworking at physical labor
- "Farmer strength" — lifts far more than expected
- Simple and kind-hearted
- Wolfram sees his kindness/generosity as embodying the Earth Mother's values
