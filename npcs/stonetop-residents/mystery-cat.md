# Mystery Cat

**Pronouns:** —  
**Home:** Stonetop (roams freely)  
**Occupation:** Unknown  
**Traits:** Black, mysterious, possibly supernatural  
**Relations:** Only observed by Gareth

## Description
A small black cat that wanders around Stonetop. Only Gareth has noticed it — no one else in town seems aware of it. It carries things, appears across town instantaneously, and watches Gareth from the shadows. Its nature is entirely unknown.

## Key Facts
- Black cat
- Only Gareth has seen/noticed it
- Appears to follow Gareth — shows up wherever he goes
- Carries strange objects
- Gareth hasn't been able to get close to it
- May appear on one side of town and then instantly appear on the other
- Stares at Gareth from shadows
- Gareth won't ask Ted about it (doesn't want to admit there's something in town he doesn't know about)
- Chases Filberta's squirrels (playful, not predatory?)

**Mystery hook.** Could be: a Fae creature, a spirit in animal form, a familiar, a manifestation of the Azure Hand's energy, or something else entirely. Gareth's private mystery.
