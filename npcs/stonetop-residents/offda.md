# Offda

**Pronouns:** she/her  
**Home:** Stonetop

## Description
The town's taxidermist, processing hides and furs brought in by the trappers. Known for knowing everything that happens in town — when the hunting party comes back, she knows everything they said and did.

## Key Facts
- Processes the furs/hides that Dilwen and other trappers bring in
- Information hub — knows all the gossip, especially from hunting parties
- Complicated relationship with Filberta
- May be a gossip source for Ted's information network
