# Faeton — Building Index

**Type:** Fae Settlement  
**Depth:** 1 (In), 3 (In), 4 (Out)  
**Population:** <50  
**Size:** Small — little to no sprawl

## Overview

A compact Fae town encountered at multiple depths along the travel route. Faeton serves as a waypoint — PCs pass through it entering the Faedom and again on the way out.

## Buildings

| # | Name | Description | Details |
|---|------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | [The Pub](pub/) | Fae drinking house. Payment is a performance — sincerity judged, not skill. Half-hearted efforts earn cold drinks and strange dreams. | See [pub patron index](pub/pub-patron-index.md) |
| 2 | [The Chime Shop](chime-shop.md) | Pearl sells chimes from behind a curtain — three at a time, wrapped in felt. Reject a batch and they're gone forever. Chimes are magical status objects with strange effects. Price: a magical or valuable item from outside the Faedom. | 3d100 generator inside |
| 3 | [The Tailor](tailor.md) | Measurement collector. Trades calling cards for precise measurements of the world. Card temporarily alters bearer's body dimensions. | |

## GM Notes
- Small enough that PCs will see the whole place quickly
- Appearing at multiple depths means PCs encounter it repeatedly — good for building familiarity and relationships
- Navigation: Deeper = South, Back = North, Sideways = E/W
