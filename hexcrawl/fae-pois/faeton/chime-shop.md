# The Chime Shop

**Location:** Faeton (Building #2)  
**Type:** Fae Shop  
**Role:** Chime seller — magical keys and show-chimes

---

## Pearl

**Appearance:** Short, round-faced, warm complexion. Hair like spun copper in a loose braid over one shoulder. Wears a fitted dress in a deep green — practical cut, good fabric, the kind of thing another woman would notice and say *"oh, that's lovely."* No jewelry except a thin chain at her throat with nothing hanging from it.

**Manner:** Greets you before you've fully entered. Already smiling. Asks your name and uses it immediately — twice in the first sentence. Talks the way someone does when they're genuinely pleased to have company after a quiet morning. Leans forward on the counter, chin in hand, asks where you've come from and what you've seen out there. Interested. Quick to laugh.

She doesn't come around the counter. Doesn't crowd you. She stays in her space behind the pale wood slab and lets you come to her.

She knows what she has. She's not selling — she's *showing*. The energy is a woman opening a box and saying *"look what I found"* and watching your face. She wants you to be impressed. She wants you to say so. If you do, she lights up. If you don't, she's unbothered — covers the felt, reaches for the next three.

**If you ask for a rejected batch back:** Her warmth vanishes. Flat. Offended. *"You are done with those."* No negotiation. The subject is closed. She'll still show you new ones, but the ease takes a minute to come back.

**On the Ard Boys:** Her warmth cools noticeably if the Tourists are mentioned or present. She doesn't refuse them service — she just becomes *precise*. Short sentences. No questions about where they've been. No leaning forward. If asked why: *"They browse. They never buy. They handle things and put them back wrong. I can hear it after."*

---

## The Shop

A single room, wider than expected from outside. Clean. Spare. Walls are smooth pale stone. Shallow shelves line them — holding nothing but candles.

**The light:** Candles everywhere — on shelves, in wall-sconces, on the counter, clustered on the floor in corners. Every color: cream, red, deep green, pale blue, black, gold. They burn steadily. They don't drip. They don't shorten. The light is warm and even — no shadows in this shop. Everything visible, nothing hidden.

**No chimes in sight.** No display cases, no hooks, no hanging inventory. The shop looks almost empty — just candles, clean stone, and the broad counter of pale wood, worn smooth. Behind it, Pearl. Behind *her*, a curtain.

---

## The Felt Ritual

When you're ready, Pearl disappears behind the curtain. Returns with three squares of dark felt, folded shut. Sets them on the counter. Opens each one. Inside: a chime, nested in the fabric.

She lets the PCs look, touch, ring if they want — *"How else would you know?"*

- **If they buy:** She wraps it in fresh felt and hands it over.
- **If they pass:** She covers all three without comment. Those three are gone. Forever.
- She'll reveal as many batches as they like, as long as they're still trading.

**Roll 3d100 on the generator below for each chime revealed** (one per column: Material, Engraving, Effect).

---

## Price

Pearl wants a **magical or valuable item from outside the Faedom.** Mortal-world goods she can't get here.

- Arcana (minor or major)
- Enchanted weapons or tools
- Rare alchemical substances
- Worked jewelry with real craftsmanship
- Anything clearly *made* by mortal hands with skill and materials

She examines offerings by holding them near a candle and watching the flame. If it flickers a certain way, she nods. If not: *"Pretty. But not enough."*

**What she won't accept:** Raw materials, food, Fae-made goods (*"I have those"*), information, favors, promises.

---

## What Chimes ARE

Chimes function as **keys**. Fae doors, locks, wards, and sealed objects respond to the correct chime — material and resonant frequency must match. A gold-inlaid door needs a gold chime at the right pitch. A bone-sealed box needs a bone chime.

Chimes are also **status objects**. Fae carry show-chimes that produce magical effects when rung — prestige, display, fashion. The weirder and more impressive the effect, the higher the status.

**Matching chimes to locks:** Locked Fae objects are typically inlaid with decorations of the same material as their chime. Symbols on or around the object might indicate the resonant frequency — knowledge of Fae music helps decode this.

## GM Notes

- The "reveal three, reject forever" mechanic puts real tension in browsing — players should feel the weight of passing
- Pearl is not threatening, not tricky — she's genuine. That's what makes her Fae: she means exactly what she says.
- Chimes-as-keys gives PCs reasons to buy specific chimes AND to come back when they find a locked thing
- If PCs find a locked Fae door/chest, note its material inlay — that's a hook back to Pearl
- Show-chimes are fun magical trinkets that don't break the game — most effects are situational and open to creative interpretation
- The Fae-tricky wording on effects is intentional: let players figure out what their chime does through use
- Source inspiration: *The Dark of Hot Springs Island*, "Random Elven Chimes" (pp. 149–155). Non-canon, heavily adapted.
