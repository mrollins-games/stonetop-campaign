# The Barkeep

**Location:** The Pub, Faeton  
**Type:** Fae NPC  
**Role:** Publican / Gatekeeper of hospitality

## Description

The proprietor of Faeton's Pub. Warm, watchful, and deeply attentive to sincerity. Serves excellent food and drink — but never for coin.

## Payment

Payment is a **performance**:
- A song
- A story
- A feat of strength
- A dance
- A cooked meal
- Any genuine offering of self

**Judges sincerity, not skill.** A terrible singer who means it gets the best seat. A gifted bard phoning it in gets cold drinks and strange dreams.

## Behavior

- Welcomes everyone warmly on arrival
- Never asks for payment upfront — serves first, then watches
- If a PC offers nothing or offers half-heartedly: cold room, bad dreams, food that doesn't satisfy
- If a PC performs with genuine effort: warm fire, excellent drink, restful sleep
- Never explains the rules directly — PCs learn by experience

## GM Notes
- Good "teach the Fae rules" NPC — PCs learn that sincerity matters more than value
- Contrasts with mortal economy where coin = service
- Repeat visits build a relationship — the Barkeep remembers what you performed last time
