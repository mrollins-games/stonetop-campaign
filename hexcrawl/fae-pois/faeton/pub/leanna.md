# Leanna (Widow Leanna)

**True Nature:** Leanan Sidhe  
**Pronouns:** she/her  
**Location:** Faeton Pub  
**Cover:** Wealthy widow, strikingly beautiful, always present but never intrusive  
**Instinct:** To feed on devotion while making beauty

## Description

A beautiful woman who frequents the Faeton Pub. Others refer to her as "Widow Leanna" — said to be ungodly wealthy. She is quiet, graceful, and magnetic. Men and women alike find reasons to linger near her table. She buys rounds but never seems drunk. She listens more than she speaks. When she does speak, people feel *seen*.

No one knows where her wealth comes from. No one has seen her husband's grave. No one questions it.

## Appearance

- Pale, dark-haired, dressed finely but simply
- Not garish or overtly seductive — her beauty is grave, sorrowful, dignified
- She doesn't approach loudly — she's just *there*, as if she always was
- Unnervingly still; more vivid than anyone else in the room
- No fairy markers visible — she looks like the most compelling version of a real woman

## The Pattern

She chooses targets with creative or passionate potential. She offers genuine brilliance — the mortal becomes extraordinary. In return, their life force drains. They grow pale, obsessive, sleepless, thin. They produce their best work while dying. They die young.

### What She Desires From Each PC

| PC | What She Offers | The Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Halpin** (Lightbearer) | Perfect clarity of faith — visions so vivid they become sermons that move entire crowds to tears | He becomes a prophet. People follow him. But the light burns from inside out — he stops eating, sleeping, seeing anything but the visions |
| **Gareth** (Seeker) | Answers. Every mystery resolves. He knows where things are, what happened, why — without searching | He becomes the smartest person in any room. But his curiosity dies — replaced by certainty. He grows listless between revelations, craving the next fix |
| **Wolfram** (Blessed) | His patron's voice becomes constant, intimate, warm — the loneliness of devotion replaced with perfect communion | He becomes serene, unshakable, radiantly faithful. But he stops needing other people entirely. The village loses him before his body fails |
| **Ted** (Judge) | Every ruling he makes is obviously correct. No one argues. No ambiguity. Perfect law from his mouth | He becomes the law made flesh. But justice without struggle becomes tyranny — people obey from awe, not respect. He becomes brittle and gaunt |
| **Filberta** (Would-Be Hero) | Deeds that actually work. Every swing lands. Every leap sticks. She becomes the hero she imagines | She becomes legendary overnight. But the gap between her real self and the gifted self widens — she can't tell which accomplishments are hers anymore |

## Rules / Taboos (Folklore)

- If a mortal **refuses** her love, she becomes enslaved to them (per Yeats) — but almost no one refuses
- A devotee can only escape by **finding a replacement** willing to take their place
- She is **not physically violent** — the drain is emotional/spiritual
- The gifts she gives are **genuinely real** — the work produced IS brilliant
- Iron weakens her glamour; sustained eye contact while naming her true nature may force a reveal

## Key Facts

- "Widow" — no one has evidence of a dead husband
- Wealth has no visible source
- Has been at the Faeton Pub longer than anyone can quite pin down
- Previous devotees? Look for brilliant locals who burned out young — artists, leaders, craftspeople who peaked and then wasted away
- She is patient. She watches. She waits for the right moment of vulnerability.

## GM Notes

- She is a **slow burn** NPC — introduce her casually across multiple sessions before anything happens
- The PCs should like her before they suspect her
- Her offers should come at moments of PC vulnerability or frustration
- The moral question isn't "stop the monster" — it's "the gifts are real and the victim doesn't want rescue"
