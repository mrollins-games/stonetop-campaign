# Faedom Flora — Herbs, Obstacles & Useful Plants

> A field guide to the living things that grow in the Faedom. Organized by depth — deeper plants are rarer, stranger, and more potent. Obstacles are flora that actively resists passage.

---

## How to Use This

### Foraging
When a PC forages or searches for useful plants, roll on the table for the **current depth**. On a 7–9 on Forage/Scout, they find one entry. On a 10+, they find one and choose a second from the same or shallower depth.

### Obstacles
When PCs travel between POIs, roll or choose an obstacle from the current depth. Obstacles get stranger deeper in — Depth 1 is merely inconvenient, Depth 4–5 is actively hostile or puzzle-like.

### Fae Law Interactions
- **Don't take what isn't given** — harvesting without asking risks offense. Asking the plant, the tree, or the nearest Fae before picking is technically correct behavior.
- **Plants here are arguably people.** Some are. Some aren't. You can't always tell. Err on the side of courtesy.
- Fae herbalists trade in cuttings, not whole plants. Ripping something out by the root is considered violent.

### Herbs & Useful Plants

| d8 | Plant | Appearance | Use | Catch |
|:---:|-------|-----------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Moonpetal | Silver-white flower, always open, faintly luminous | Brewed into tea: grants dreamless, restful sleep (heal 1 extra on Make Camp) | Wilts instantly if picked during the day. Must be harvested at night. |
| 2 | Gossipvine | Thin green vine with heart-shaped leaves that rustle without wind | Pressed against a wound: stops bleeding (stabilizes a dying PC) | Whispers what it's heard to anyone who holds it. Secrets overheard nearby may be repeated. |
| 3 | Thornberry | Red-black berries on wickedly thorned stems | Eaten raw: nourishing (counts as a ration). Fermented: strong Fae wine | The thorns are mild iron — they sting Fae. Carrying them openly marks you as rude. |
| 4 | Breathmoss | Pale green moss on north-facing stones, expands and contracts like lungs | Packed over the mouth: lets you breathe in airless or underwater spaces (10 minutes) | Must be kept damp. Dries out in one day. Cannot be stored. |
| 5 | Trail Sage | Low grey-green herb growing at path edges, smells of rain | Burned: the smoke points toward the nearest path or road | The smoke also signals your presence to anything within a quarter-mile. |
| 6 | Weeping Willow Sap | Golden amber leaking from willows near water | Applied to skin: soothes burns, insect bites, and mild poisons | The willow remembers who took from it. Take too much and it leans toward you when you pass — disconcerting. |
| 7 | Cloverchime | Four-leaf clover with a tiny bell-shaped flower at center | Carried: advantage on next roll to resist Fae glamour or illusion | Only works once per clover. Crumbles after use. Hard to find — always at the edge of your vision. |
| 8 | Rootbread Fungus | Flat beige mushroom growing on fallen logs, smells of fresh bread | Edible raw (counts as a ration). Tastes exactly like warm bread. | Grows back overnight if you leave the stem. Taking the stem kills it permanently — the log mourns (creaking, leaning). |

### Obstacles

| d6 | Obstacle | Description | How to Pass |
|:---:|----------|------------|-------------|
| 1 | Bramble Wall | Dense thorned hedge, 8 feet tall, completely blocking the path. Natural but suspiciously deliberate. | Cut through (slow, loud, damages gear), go around (adds time), or ask politely ("May I pass?") — it parts if you use the right tone. |
| 2 | Territorial Roots | Tree roots raised above ground, shifting slightly, tripping anyone who moves too fast. | Walk slowly and deliberately. Running triggers more roots. Patience is the key. |
| 3 | Pollen Cloud | A grove of flowers releasing thick golden pollen. Beautiful but makes eyes water and lungs itch. | Hold breath and push through (Defy Danger CON), wait for wind to shift (1 hour), or cover face with wet cloth. |
| 4 | Leaning Trees | Two massive trees leaning toward each other over the path, almost touching. Gap is tight — single file, duck low. | Squeeze through. Anyone in heavy armor or carrying large packs gets stuck briefly. Trees seem to enjoy the inconvenience. |
| 5 | Stream Without a Ford | A stream that's too wide to jump, too deep to wade (waist-high), and too fast to swim safely. | Find stepping stones (hidden — requires Scout), build a crossing (time + noise), or follow the bank until it narrows. |
| 6 | The Wrong Clearing | You've been here before. This clearing looks identical to one you passed 20 minutes ago. You're going in circles. | Stop. Sit. Wait. The path reveals itself to the patient. Or: turn your coat inside-out (Fae lore — breaks spatial tricks). |

### Herbs & Useful Plants

| d8 | Plant | Appearance | Use | Catch |
|:---:|-------|-----------|-----|-------|

| 5 | Honeythorn | A bush with thorns that drip golden honey instead of drawing blood | The honey: delicious, slightly intoxicating, makes Fae friendly toward you for an hour | Technically belongs to whatever Fae tends that bush. You're stealing. The Fae may not notice immediately. May. |

| d6 | Obstacle | Description | How to Pass |

| 2 | Singing Thorns | A hedge of thorns that hum a melody. Beautiful. Getting closer makes you want to listen rather than pass. Will save or waste an hour just... listening. | Plug ears, have someone pull you through, or sing BACK (if your song is better, they go quiet in respect). |

| 4 | Mushroom Maze | A field of waist-high mushrooms that all look identical. | 5 | Courtesy Gate | Two trees grown together in an arch. You can see through it. But walking through without knocking on both trunks first deposits you back where you started. | Knock twice. Wait. Say "thank you" to no one visible. Then walk through. Anyone who doesn't do all three steps loops. |
| 6 | Nesting Swarm | A section of path thick with web and cocoons. Something lives here — not hostile, just occupying space. Hundreds of tiny silk bundles. | Walk carefully (Defy Danger DEX), clearing webs as you go. Or: wait for the occupant to leave (sunset — they hunt at dusk). Destroying the nests works but the occupant returns furious. |

## GM Notes

### Scarcity Progression
- **Depth 1:** Common — PCs find these easily. Multiple per forage attempt.
- **Depth 2:** Uncommon — one per successful forage. May need to search specifically.
- **Depth 3:** Rare — requires dedicated effort, time, and often a cost (noise, attention, risk).
- **Depth 4:** Very rare — finding one is a minor event. Usually guarded, hidden, or in dangerous locations.
- **Depth 5:** Legendary — you don't find these. They find you. Or they don't exist until you need them.

### Foraging Mechanics (Stonetop-Compatible)
- **Forage (WIS):** Roll when spending time gathering.
  - **10+:** Find one herb from current depth table. Choose which.
  - **7–9:** Find one herb from current depth table. GM rolls randomly.
  - **6-:** Find nothing useful, or find something but attract attention (noise, Fae notice, disturb something).
- **Know Things (WIS/INT):** Can identify a plant already found. On 10+, know its use AND its catch. On 7–9, know one but not the other.

### Obstacle Philosophy
- Depth 1–2 obstacles are **physical** — strength, patience, cleverness solve them.
- Depth 3 obstacles are **conceptual** — they test perception, courage, or values.
- Depth 4 obstacles are **metaphysical** — they test resolve, identity, or sacrifice.
- Depth 5 obstacles are **existential** — they test whether you have the right to be here at all.

### Integration with Other Tables
- If a PC uses Trail Sage (Depth 1) to find a path, it leads to the nearest POI in the current depth — use the master POI table in [fae-travel.md](../fae-travel.md).
- Herbs make good Fae Toll payments (see [rollable-tables.md](rollable-tables.md) — Fae Demands table).
- Aggressive flora obstacles can replace or supplement travel encounters from [encounters.md](encounters.md).
- The Verdict Leaf (Depth 3) ties directly to the Hollow Court archive.
