# Hooks to Inject Into Play

> GM checklist — things that need to be seeded into upcoming sessions to start encounters/arcs rolling.

---

## Active Hooks

| # | Hook | What It Starts | Trigger / How to Inject | Status |
|---|------|---------------|------------------------|--------|
| 1 | Gareth's vision of a woman sweeping a tomb | Blush-of-Dawn encounter (Beat 1 — Stream) | Remind Danny R. of the vision during travel near water in Depth 1-2. She's at a stream, not the tomb. | Not yet |
| 2 | A lone Fae haggling with itself on the path | The Would-Be Merchant → Wandering Market | Drop during travel. Fae needs three things: a gift (freely given), a glamour (illusion worth selling), a prize (won in contest). Solving all three opens the Market. | Not yet |
| 3 | Four tall, amused strangers at the Pub | The Tourists (Ard Sidhe from Ard Dún) | Present when PCs first enter the Faeton Pub. They're watching, sketching, finding mortals gently entertaining. Can escalate to social pressure, information trades, or mild antagonism. | Not yet |

---

## Notes

- **Order doesn't matter** — inject whichever fits the moment.
- **Beat 1 (Blush-of-Dawn)** is time-sensitive only in that it should happen before deeper Faedom travel. Depth 1-2, near water.
- **The Would-Be Merchant** can happen anywhere on the road. Works best when the party is between destinations and could use a distraction.
- **The Tourists** are a Faeton fixture — they'll be there whenever the PCs visit the Pub. No rush, but they're great for first-visit flavor and establishing that deeper Fae exist.
