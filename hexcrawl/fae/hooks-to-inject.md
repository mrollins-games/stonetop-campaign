## Notes

- **Order doesn't matter** — inject whichever fits the moment.
- **Beat 1 (Blush-of-Dawn)** is time-sensitive only in that it should happen before deeper Faedom travel. Depth 1-2, near water.
- **The Would-Be Merchant** can happen anywhere on the road. Works best when the party is between destinations and could use a distraction.
- **The Tourists** are a Faeton fixture — they'll be there whenever the PCs visit the Pub. No rush, but they're great for first-visit flavor and establishing that deeper Fae exist.
- **Halpin's gold ring** — the vision mirrors the existing Fetch concept (radiant self, kneeling crowd) but this is the FIRST encounter with it, not the Fetch proper. The ring is the bait. The ambiguity (Helior or him?) is the poison. This gives Dan G. a new minor arcana and opens his temptation arc.
- **Ted's court vision** — plays on his secret (he KNOWS the contract is broken). The vision shows him he could FIX it through law, not deception. The temptation: reconvene the Hollow Court for real. The cost: whatever the Court demands in exchange for reassembling (and what happens when his complicity with Axel's wood comes to light IN court).
- **Filberta's sacrifice vision** — gives Kari exactly what she wants (heroic death saving friends) and then shows the cost: you become Fae, immortal, legendary — but anchored. You can never leave this forest. You saved them but you lost them. The Would-Be Hero becomes the Would-Be-Remembered-Hero-Who-Is-Trapped-Here-Forever.
