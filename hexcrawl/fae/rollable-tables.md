## 1. Fae Demands (d20)

**When to Roll:** A Fae blocks the path, guards a threshold, demands payment, or offers a bargain. Works for tolls, trade, Bargain Door encounters, or any Fae insisting on exchange. Demands follow Fae logic — absurd but sincere.

| d20 | Demand | Or Else (if refused/broken) |
|:---:|--------|-----|
| 1 | A step tax. One memory per step taken in their presence. (They count.) | You forget how to walk home. Your feet carry you somewhere wrong. |
| 2 | A shadow toll. Leave your shadow here until you return. (It peels off the ground.) | Your shadow acts independently — walks ahead, gestures to others, wanders off. |
| 3 | A name fee. Not YOUR name — the name of someone who wronged you. | They take a name anyway — from the nearest person you love. That person forgets you for a day. |
| 4 | A noise tax. You were too loud arriving. Pay one song, performed now, or owe silence for the next hour. | Your voice becomes unbearably loud for a day. Everything you say echoes through the Faedom. |
| 5 | A weight toll. Leave behind the heaviest thing you carry. Emotional weight counts. | The weight doubles. Literally. Your pack, your armor, your guilt. |
| 6 | A color fee. Something red. (They don't specify what. Anything red.) | Color drains from one item you love. It becomes grey forever. |
| 7 | A future tax. Promise to do one thing for them. They'll collect later. (They won't say what.) | They collect NOW — something you'd never have promised. You still owe it. |
| 8 | A question toll. Answer one question truthfully. (The question is always uncomfortably personal.) | They ask in front of everyone next time. The question is worse. |
| 9 | A warmth fee. Breathe on their hands until they say stop. | Cold follows you. Fires won't light near you until you do a kindness unprompted. |
| 10 | A laughter tax. Make them laugh. Genuinely. They are a difficult audience. | They make YOU the joke. Next d6 Fae you meet already know an embarrassing story about you. |
| 11 | A time toll. Sit here with them in silence for one hour. No speaking. No leaving. | Time skips. You lose a day and arrive late to whatever mattered most. |
| 12 | A companion fee. They want to walk with you to the next place. They won't leave until you arrive. | They follow anyway — but silently, and other Fae treat you as if you kidnapped them. |
| 13 | A tooth. Any tooth. Not necessarily yours. | Toothache that nothing cures. Gets worse every day until paid. |
| 14 | An apology for something you didn't do. Must sound sincere. | Guilt settles on you. Other Fae smell it. They assume you did something terrible. |
| 15 | A story you've never told anyone. It must be true. | They invent one about you and spread it. It's worse than the truth. |
| 16 | A trade of burdens. Carry their sack (impossibly heavy, smells of wet earth) for one hour. | Your burdens become visible — floating behind you as spectral luggage. Everything you worry about, on display. |
| 17 | A favor owed to a third party. Not to them — to someone they name who lives deeper in. | The third party arrives to collect in the worst possible moment. |
| 18 | One hour of your sleep. Tonight. They'll be there to take it. | Insomnia for three nights. On the third, you hallucinate Fae watching you. (They are.) |
| 19 | An object you made with your own hands. (Counts even if crude — whittled stick, braided cord.) | Your hands feel clumsy for a week. -1 ongoing to anything requiring craft or fine work until you make something beautiful. |
| 20 | Nothing. They wave you through. "You'll know when you owe me." | The debt arrives at the worst possible dramatic moment. GM holds this in reserve. |

### Geas Escalation (for bargains and pacts)

When a Fae offers **power** in exchange for a demand, the cost is daily and escalates:
- **Day 1-7:** Minor inconvenience (the demand applies once per day in a small way)
- **Day 8-14:** The demand warps behavior (others notice something wrong)
- **Day 15+:** The demand becomes your defining trait (you ARE the payment now)
- **Broken geas:** The power inverts. Whatever it gave, it now takes. Permanently until amends are made.

## 2. Strange Items (d20)

**When to Roll:** When PCs find something left on the trail, loot a Fae place, browse Faeton's Chime Shop, or receive a gift. Check the Type column to know the economy: gifts are free, trade items cost something non-monetary, loot is found in the wild.

| d20 | Item | Type | Effect / Catch |
|:---:|------|:----:|----------------|

| 2 | A folded leaf containing exactly the herb someone needs. | Gift | Always the right herb. Never enough for a second dose. |
| 3 | A tiny compass of twig and spiderweb. | Gift | Points toward the nearest anchor stone — once. Then crumbles. |
| 4 | A cup that fills with something warm when held with both hands. | Trade | Heals 1 harm per drink. Drinker speaks in rhyme until dawn. Cup wants a secret. |
| 5 | A woven bracelet, still warm. | Gift | Invisible to the next hostile creature encountered. Then unravels. |
| 6 | A jar of preserved starlight. | Trade | 10 minutes of perfect illumination through walls. Costs a childhood memory per use. |
| 7 | A bird-bone flute with three notes. | Trade | Each note does one thing: calm (1st), summon (2nd), banish (3rd). After all three played, flute shatters. Costs a song you love — you forget it forever. |
| 8 | A doorframe of mushrooms, standing alone. | Loot | Walk through: arrive at the last place you camped. One use. Smells of where you're going. |
| 9 | An hourglass that runs with honey instead of sand. | Trade | While running, time moves half-speed in 10ft radius. Takes one hour of YOUR time per minute used. You age. |
| 10 | A sealed envelope. Inside: "Wait here. We are rearranging." | Gift | If the party waits 10 minutes, the next POI is one depth-layer closer. |
| 11 | A mirror the size of a palm. Shows the viewer as a Fae creature. | Loot | The creature shown is what you'd become if you stayed too long. Sell it to a vain Fae for a major favor. |
| 12 | A key carved from a frozen sigh. | Loot | Opens one Fae lock. Any. Even metaphorical ones. Melts after use. |
| 13 | A ball of thread that always leads somewhere. Never where you expected. | Trade | Follow it: you arrive at what you NEED, not what you WANT. Costs: you can't turn back once you start following. |
| 14 | A thimble that's always full of honey. | Trade | The honey heals, soothes, and makes Fae trust you. But it's technically stolen from someone deeper in. They'll notice eventually. |
| 15 | A feather that falls UP. | Loot | Drop it: it rises to the ceiling/sky and sticks. Mark a location permanently — even in pocket dimensions that shift. |
| 16 | A candle that shows the room as it was one hour ago when lit. | Trade | See the past of any room. Cannot be un-seen. Some rooms contain things that shouldn't be witnessed. Costs: one truth you believe is overwritten by what you see. |

| 19 | A coin that always comes back. Always. Even if given away, buried, or thrown into the sea. | Loot | Useful for marking, tracking, annoying. But: whoever had it last knows where you are. |
| 20 | An acorn that whispers the name of the nearest tree when held to the ear. | Gift | Trees have names here. Knowing a tree's name means it might help you. Might. |

## 4. Absurd Fae Openers (d12)

**When to Roll:** When PCs meet a random Fae NPC who isn't pre-written and you need an instant personality. Use as their opening line. Deliver with total sincerity. The Fae is not joking.

| d12 | Opening Line |
|:---:|-------------|
| 1 | "What's your name? How do you spell that? No — how do you *smell* that. Say it again. Slower. With your teeth." |
| 2 | "I see you have an impressive forthwrick. Yes, just there — behind your thrombulatte. Don't touch it, you'll ruin it." |
| 3 | "Stop copying me. I was breathing before you even arrived. I have witnesses." |
| 4 | "Forgive me — I seem to have forgotten my name. Could you hold this while I look for it?" *(hands PC a warm, pulsing acorn)* |
| 5 | "You should be ashamed of yourself. It would be a good name for you. 'Ashamed Ofyour Self.' And you —" *(points to next PC)* "— you should be Basil." |
| 6 | "Whatever it is you're looking for, I'm sure it doesn't exist. I checked." |
| 7 | "Excuse me — why am I dreaming you? You aren't the least bit interesting." |
| 8 | *(observes any PC action)* "Do you always behave so offensively? In my country that gesture means you've declared a minor war." |
| 9 | "Interloper! View my invention!" *(points at a completely ordinary rock)* |
| 10 | "Ah! Finally. You're late. I've been waiting since before you decided to come." |
| 11 | "I shan't speak to you until you've apologized." *(will not explain for what)* |
| 12 | "Good. You'll do. Stand there. Don't move. I need to measure the space between your thoughts." |

## 8. Atmosphere Generator

### Roll once per column:

**What's Wrong Here (d12):**

| d12 | Phenomenon |
|:---:|-----------|
| 1 | The light comes from the wrong direction. Shadows point toward the sun. |
| 2 | Everything is slightly too large — leaves the size of plates, mushrooms as tall as children. |
| 3 | The place looks abandoned — but it's clean. Someone tends this. You never see them. |
| 4 | Sound arrives late. You hear your own footsteps from three seconds ago. |
| 5 | There's a building (or ruin) that shouldn't exist. It matches no known architecture. |
| 6 | The ground is warm. Not unpleasantly — like sleeping stone that was in the sun. It's midnight. |

| 3 | Something from outside the Faedom has gotten in. It doesn't belong. The Fae are panicking quietly. |
| 4 | A bargain made generations ago is coming due. Someone's grandchild owes. |
| 5 | A POI has moved. It's now where a different POI was. Both parties are furious. |

| d10 | Why it can't wait |
|:---:|-----------------|
| 1 | Someone will die (or be permanently changed) by sunrise. |
| 2 | The situation is getting worse every hour — compound interest on a bad debt. |
| 3 | The PCs are being blamed for it (incorrectly — probably). |
| 4 | A powerful NPC has given an ultimatum with a visible deadline. |

| d10 | What makes it hard |
|:---:|-------------------|

## GM Notes — Using These Tables

### Philosophy
- These are **improvisation fuel, not scripture**. Reroll or adapt if a result doesn't fit.
- The **Trigger Guide** at the top is your in-session cheat sheet. Scan it when a situation arises.
- Generators (Creature, Atmosphere, Hook) use **multiple columns** — roll once per column, read left-to-right.
- Single tables (Demands, Openers, News) are standalone — one roll, one result.

### Session Rhythm
1. **Before play:** Roll Faedom News (d20) for background color. Optionally roll Hook Generator if you need a plot thread.
2. **During travel:** Use encounters.md for the event, then Creature Generator (Activity + Attitude) for any creature.
3. **At new locations:** Atmosphere Generator (Phenomenon + Sensory) layered on depth atmosphere.
4. **At thresholds/negotiations:** Fae Demands (d20) for toll. Full d20 for major encounters, Quick Toll (d6) for minor.
5. **At Faeton:** Notice Tree for flavor. Strange Items for shopping. Absurd Openers for random NPCs.

### Key Principles
- **Named NPCs have their own voice.** Don't use Absurd Openers for Barkeep, Leanna, Tourists, Lord Wokn, etc.
- **Activity should ALWAYS be rolled** for creature encounters. Takes 2 seconds, completely changes tone.
- **"Or Else" on Demands is a GM move**, not automatic. Use it when the PCs refuse OR when they break a bargain later.
- **Strange Items type matters:** Gifts appear freely. Trade items are in shops or offered with conditions. Loot is found in the wild and may have unknown provenance (read: possible theft consequences).
- **Hook Generator creates obligation.** Don't roll it unless you want something active in play. Faedom News is safe background — Hooks demand response.
