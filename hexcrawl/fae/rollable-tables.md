# Faedom — Rollable Tables

Quick-reference tables for improvisation at the table. Each table has a **When to Roll** instruction.

---

## 1. Doing What? (d20)

**When to Roll:** Every time a Fae creature from the [Fae table](wandering-fae.md) is encountered. Roll to determine what it's doing when the PCs find it. This adds texture and prevents every encounter from defaulting to "it notices you."

| d20 | Activity |
|:---:|----------|
| 1 | Sleeping |
| 2 | Eating |
| 3 | Wounded |
| 4 | Lost |
| 5 | Fleeing something |
| 6 | Performing a ritual |
| 7 | In combat with another Fae |
| 8 | Making art (carving, singing, weaving) |
| 9 | Patrolling territory |
| 10 | Meditating or completely still |
| 11 | Laboring at a task |
| 12 | Territorial display (marking, posturing, warning) |
| 13 | Altered state (drunk, enchanted, dreaming awake) |
| 14 | Diplomacy with another creature |
| 15 | Hunting or gathering |
| 16 | Mourning or weeping |
| 17 | Celebrating alone |
| 18 | Hiding (poorly) |
| 19 | Watching the PCs (already aware) |
| 20 | Doing something deeply mundane (grooming, defecating, counting leaves) |

## 2. Fae Toll Demands (d12)

**When to Roll:** When PCs encounter a Fae blocking the path, guarding a threshold, or demanding payment to pass. Works for the Bargain Door, bridge encounters, or any Fae who insists on a toll. The demands follow Fae logic — absurd but sincere.

| d12 | Demand |
|:---:|--------|
| 1 | A step tax. One memory per step taken in their presence. (They count.) |
| 2 | A shadow toll. Leave your shadow here until you return. (It peels off the ground.) |
| 3 | A name fee. Not YOUR name — the name of someone who wronged you. |
| 4 | A noise tax. You were too loud arriving. Pay one song, performed now, or owe silence for the next hour. |
| 5 | A weight toll. Leave behind the heaviest thing you carry. Emotional weight counts. |
| 6 | A color fee. Something red. (They don't specify what. Anything red.) |
| 7 | A future tax. Promise to do one thing for them. They'll collect later. (They won't say what.) |
| 8 | A question toll. Answer one question truthfully. (The question is always uncomfortably personal.) |
| 9 | A warmth fee. Breathe on their hands until they say stop. |
| 10 | A laughter tax. Make them laugh. Genuinely. They are a difficult audience. |
| 11 | A time toll. Sit here with them in silence for one hour. No speaking. No leaving. |
| 12 | A companion fee. They want to walk with you to the next place. They won't leave until you arrive. |

## 3. Trail Offerings (d10)

**When to Roll:** When PCs are lost, struggling, or have been respectful of Fae law — something helpful appears on the trail. These are unsigned gifts from the Faedom itself (or from Fae watching unseen). Never explained. Never repeated. Roll when you want to reward good behavior or ease a hard session without breaking tension.

| d10 | Offering |
|:---:|----------|
| 1 | A smooth stone with a single warm word carved into it. Holding it grants dreamless sleep tonight. |
| 2 | A folded leaf containing exactly the herb someone in the party needs. |
| 3 | A tiny compass made of twig and spiderweb. Points toward the nearest anchor stone — once. Then crumbles. |
| 4 | A cup of something warm sitting on a stump. Drinking it heals 1 harm but the drinker speaks in rhyme until dawn. |
| 5 | A note pinned to a tree in leaf-script: the opposite of useful advice. (Read it backward and it's correct.) |
| 6 | A woven bracelet on the path. Wearing it makes the wearer invisible to one specific creature — the next hostile one encountered. Then it unravels. |
| 7 | A jar of preserved starlight. Crack it open: 10 minutes of perfect illumination in all directions, even through walls. Fae within earshot wince. |
| 8 | A bird lands on the nearest shoulder and sings the melody of the safest path forward. Flies away when the path changes. |
| 9 | A doorframe made of mushrooms, standing alone. Walk through it: you arrive at the last place you camped. One use. |
| 10 | A tiny sealed envelope. Inside: "Wait here. We are rearranging." If the party waits 10 minutes, the next POI they seek is one depth-layer closer. |

## 4. Absurd Fae Openers (d12)

**When to Roll:** When PCs meet a random Fae NPC who isn't pre-written and you need an instant personality. Use as their opening line. Deliver with total sincerity. The Fae is not joking.

| d12 | Opening Line |
|:---:|-------------|
| 1 | "What's your name? How do you spell that? No — how do you *smell* that. Say it again. Slower. With your teeth." |
| 2 | "I see you have an impressive forthwrick. Yes, just there — behind your thrombulatte. Don't touch it, you'll ruin it." |
| 3 | "Stop copying me. I was breathing before you even arrived. I have witnesses." |
| 4 | "Forgive me — I seem to have forgotten my name. Could you hold this while I look for it?" *(hands PC a warm, pulsing acorn)* |
| 5 | "You should be ashamed of yourself. It would be a good name for you. 'Ashamed Ofyour Self.' And you —" *(points to next PC)* "— you should be Basil." |
| 6 | "Whatever it is you're looking for, I'm sure it doesn't exist. I checked." |
| 7 | "Excuse me — why am I dreaming you? You aren't the least bit interesting." |
| 8 | *(observes any PC action)* "Do you always behave so offensively? In my country that gesture means you've declared a minor war." |
| 9 | "Interloper! View my invention!" *(points at a completely ordinary rock)* |
| 10 | "Ah! Finally. You're late. I've been waiting since before you decided to come." |
| 11 | "I shan't speak to you until you've apologized." *(will not explain for what)* |
| 12 | "Good. You'll do. Stand there. Don't move. I need to measure the space between your thoughts." |

## 5. Notice Tree Messages (Generator)

**When to Roll:** When PCs visit the Notice Tree in Faeton (or the Courier's Rest POI). Roll once on each column and read the message aloud. Messages are pinned on leaves in Fae script. Some are ancient.

### Roll once per column:

**Addressed To (d8):**

| d8 | To... |
|:---:|-------|
| 1 | "...my most vigorous and noble..." |
| 2 | "...the festering carcass known to men as..." |
| 3 | "...your esteemed eminence..." |
| 4 | "...my beloved and devoted..." |
| 5 | "...the one who still owes me..." |
| 6 | "...whoever finds this..." |
| 7 | "...my first and prodigal..." |
| 8 | "...the mortal with the [describe a PC's most visible feature]..." |

**From (d8):**

| d8 | Sender |
|:---:|--------|
| 1 | A name no one recognizes, signed with excessive flourish |
| 2 | Lord Wokn (official seal, cold tone) |
| 3 | "Your loving aunt" (no aunt is known) |
| 4 | The Courier's Rest waystone itself |
| 5 | An Ellyllon collective (illegible co-signatures) |
| 6 | Liksa's court (thread-sealed, smells of silk) |
| 7 | "Unsigned. You know why." |
| 8 | A PC's own name (in handwriting they don't recognize as theirs) |

**Content (d10):**

| d10 | Message Body |
|:---:|-------------|
| 1 | Seek the counsel of [random Fae NPC]. They know about the [nearest unvisited POI]. |
| 2 | Under no circumstances should you speak to [random Fae NPC]. |
| 3 | I have sent three copies via three routes. If you're reading this, the other two failed. |
| 4 | The [item/person] you lost is in [wrong direction]. Go quickly. |
| 5 | It's in the third drawer down behind the dovecote. You'll understand when you see it. |
| 6 | Whatever they offered you — refuse. Whatever they asked of you — accept. |
| 7 | A map that is clearly wrong but very confidently drawn. One landmark is real. |
| 8 | Destroy this after reading. *(The leaf resists being torn.)* |
| 9 | A recipe for something impossible (soup made from a whispered secret, bread baked in moonlight). |
| 10 | "You were right. I'm sorry. Please come home." *(addressed to no one present — or is it?)* |

## 6. Faedom News (d20)

**When to Roll:** At the start of a session set in the Faedom, or when PCs ask "what's happening?" Roll to determine background events. These are rumors heard from Fae, overheard in Faeton's pub, or noticed in the landscape. They create a living world and may become hooks.

| d20 | Event |
|:---:|-------|
| 1 | A procession of Aos Sí was seen heading [direction]. Someone owes something. |
| 2 | Lord Wokn's encampment has moved. No one knows where — or admits knowing. |
| 3 | A duel between two Fae over a trivial insult. Spectators are taking sides. The insult: one called the other's hat "adequate." |
| 4 | Liksa has commissioned something new. Thread-Bound have been seen carrying bolts of impossible fabric toward the Sunken Sette. |
| 5 | The Revel Clearing's music changed key last night. Old Fae are unsettled. That key hasn't been played since the war. |
| 6 | Something was stolen from the Bear of Winter. He's in a mood. Leaving his domain is... inadvisable right now. |
| 7 | The Ellyllon are giggling louder than usual. They found something. Or someone. |
| 8 | A new door appeared in Faeton overnight. No one claims to own it. It's locked. |
| 9 | The Singing Rade passed through twice in one day. Unprecedented. Some say it means a new Fae was born. Others say it means one died. |
| 10 | A mortal was seen deeper than Depth 3. Alone. Smiling. The Pub's barkeep won't talk about it. |
| 11 | The Notice Tree has shed all its leaves. New ones are growing — but the messages on them are addressed to people who haven't arrived yet. |
| 12 | One of the Tourists hasn't returned from the Hotsprings Rivera. The other three don't seem worried. |
| 13 | A Hollow Fae was spotted near [nearest POI]. Other Fae are giving the area wide berth. |
| 14 | The Mirror Glade is reflecting somewhere it shouldn't. People who look in see a place none of them recognize. |
| 15 | Wokn sent a courier. The courier hasn't arrived. The message was about [random PC's name]. |
| 16 | The Endless Masons have stopped building. First time in recorded history. They're just... standing there. |
| 17 | A bargain was broken. Everyone felt it. Like a bell rung wrong. No one knows whose. |
| 18 | The Safari Company was turned away from a POI. They're furious and offering favors to anyone who'll get them in. |
| 19 | The atmosphere at Depth 1 changed — birdsong stopped mid-phrase and hasn't resumed. (Temporary — returns next session.) |
| 20 | Time skipped. Everyone in the Faedom lost a day. No one remembers what happened during it. |

## GM Notes — Using These Tables

- **Don't roll all of them every session.** Pick 1-2 that fit the moment.
- **Faedom News** is best as a session-opener. Roll before play.
- **Doing What?** should be rolled every time a creature is encountered — it takes 2 seconds and completely changes the tone.
- **Fae Toll** and **Trail Offerings** are situational — use when the fiction calls for it.
- **Absurd Openers** are for unnamed NPCs only. Named NPCs (Barkeep, Leanna, Tourists, etc.) have their own voices.
- **Notice Tree** is for when PCs actively look at the tree or the Courier's Rest. Don't force it.
- These tables are **inspiration, not scripture**. If a result doesn't fit, reroll or adapt.
