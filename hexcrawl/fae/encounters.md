# Faedom — Encounters

Non-combat events that happen during travel or at camp. Roll or choose as appropriate.

| # | Name | Road | Camp | Description |
|---|------|:----:|:----:|-------------|
| 1 | The Singing Rade | ✓ | | A joyful singing procession lines up small to tall and passes through. PCs can leave once it passes. Read rapidly for chaotic feel: **Tiny:** Seed-pod rattlers · Cricket-legged fiddlers · Mushroom-capped toddlers · Glowing sprites **Medium:** Fox-tailed flutists · Vine-wrapped acrobats · Moth-winged lantern-carriers · Moss-backed wanderers **Large:** Masked singers · Antlered pipers · Tree-limbed dancers · Horned drummers |
| 2 | The Would-Be Merchant | ✓ | | A lone Fae desperate to convene the Wandering Market. Needs three things: a gift (given freely), a glamour (illusion worth selling), and a prize (won in contest). Help them → the Market appears and you're owed a favor. |
| 3 | The Safari Company | ✓ | | Sixteen Fae tourists — four ranks deep, four to a row. "A-TTEN-TION! Ready for inspection, gents!" Interested in *attractions* and memorabilia. **Favor:** Direct them to sights → 1. Compliment collection + listen → 1. **Spend:** Trade 1:1 for Item Value from their wagon. |
| 4 | The Bargain Door | ✓ | | A door standing alone in a clearing, slightly ajar. Peek through and you see home. Step through and you're somewhere else entirely — and the door is gone behind you. Exploits homesickness. |
| 5 | The Lantern Drift | ✓ | ✓ | Floating lights that pull you off the trail step by step. Not malicious, just compelling. You follow without deciding to. Each step feels reasonable. By the time you notice, the trail is gone. |
| 6 | The Resting Stump | | ✓ | A mossy seat that looks perfect for a weary traveller. Sit down and you can't stand. The forest grows around you while you "rest." Others find you days later, smiling. |
| 7 | The Watcher | | ✓ | A pair of eyes in the canopy, always there when you look up. Never blinks. Never moves. Never does anything. But every clearing it watches feels wrong, and no one camps there twice. |
