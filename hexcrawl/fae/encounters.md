# Faedom — Encounters

Encounters separate from POIs. These happen during travel, at camp, or between locations. Roll or choose as appropriate.

---

## Travel Encounters

| # | Name | Trigger | Description |
|---|------|---------|-------------|
| E1 | The Singing Rade | Instead of leaving first POI | A joyful singing procession lines up small to tall and passes through. PCs can leave once it passes. Read rapidly for chaotic feel: **Tiny/Underfoot:** Seed-pod rattlers (shaking themselves as percussion) · Cricket-legged fiddlers (thumb-sized, perched on shoulders) · Mushroom-capped toddlers (waddling, clapping off-beat) · Glowing sprites (capering, chaotic, weaving between legs) **Medium:** Fox-tailed flutists (quick, darting) · Vine-wrapped acrobats (tumbling, cartwheeling through) · Moth-winged lantern-carriers (holding glowing orbs, drifting above) · Moss-backed wanderers (slow, swaying, humming bass notes) **Large/Prominent:** Masked singers (bone/leaf/bark masks, voices impossibly clear) · Antlered pipers (fur-clad, wild-haired) · Tree-limbed dancers (bark-skinned, branch-fingers, moving like wind) · Horned drummers (ram-horned, bodhráns, bringing up the rear) |
| E2 | The Would-Be Merchant | Random | A lone Fae desperate to convene the Wandering Market. Needs three things: a gift (given freely, no trick), a glamour (illusion/disguise worth selling), and a prize (won in contest). Help them → the Market appears and you're owed a favor by a merchant. |
| E3 | The Safari Company | Random | Sixteen Fae tourists on safari — four ranks deep, four to a row. One shouts "A-TTEN-TION! Ready for inspection, gents!" Interested in *attractions* and memorabilia. Haul an open-top wagon of loot. **Hold Favor:** Direct them to sights → 1 Favor. Compliment collection + listen to acquisition story → 1 Favor. **Spend Favor:** Trade 1:1 for Item Value from wagon. PCs can pool. |
| E4 | The Bargain Door | Random | A door standing alone in a clearing, slightly ajar. Peek through and you see home. Step through and you're somewhere else entirely — and the door is gone behind you. Exploits homesickness. |
| E5 | The Lantern Drift | Random, off-trail | Floating lights that pull you off the trail step by step. Not malicious, just compelling. You follow without deciding to. Each step feels reasonable. By the time you notice, the trail is gone. |

## Camp Encounters

| # | Name | Trigger | Description |
|---|------|---------|-------------|
| C1 | The Resting Stump | Making camp | A mossy seat that looks perfect for a weary traveller. Sit down and you can't stand. The forest grows around you while you "rest." Others find you days later, smiling. |
| C2 | The Watcher | Making camp | A pair of eyes in the canopy, always there when you look up. Never blinks. Never moves. Never does anything. But every clearing it watches feels wrong, and no one camps there twice. |

## Creature Encounters

| # | Name | Pattern | Instinct | Description |
|---|------|---------|----------|-------------|
| F1 | Root-Keeper | Merrow | to trade warmth for buried secrets | Lives in hollow trees. Trades warmth and memories for secrets buried in the forest. Keeps "lost things" tangled in roots — objects, names, promises. What you trade away stays down there. |
| F2 | Thread-Bound | Selkie | to reclaim stolen freedom | A Fae sewn into a garment by Liksa's court. Unravel the thread, free the person. But whoever holds the spool controls them. They serve — hollow-eyed, obedient — until the thread is cut or the fabric unmade. Ties into Liksa's textile domain. |
| F3 | Canopy Hunter | Each-Uisge | to gorge on mortal presumption | Something in the high branches. More savage than anything at ground level. Drops, devours, gone. Deepwood only — never seen at shallow depths. No bargains. No warnings. Just silence, then weight from above. |
| F4 | Blight Sower | Tarbh Uisge | to seed growth with a foreign will | Corrupts growth itself. Gardens grow wrong, fruit tastes of ash, saplings twist into something else's shape. Parasitic — doesn't destroy, *replaces*. The new growth serves something. |
| F5 | The Oath-Keeper | Gwragedd y Llyn | to punish careless affection with irreversible loss | Offers a lasting pact — protection, guidance, partnership — with impossible-seeming conditions. Generous, warm, genuine. Break one condition and they're gone. Irreversibly. No second chances, no negotiation. The loss is the point. |
