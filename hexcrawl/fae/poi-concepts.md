# Fae Territory — POI Concepts

Working list of points of interest for the Faedom (F4, F6, F8, E9, etc.). Not yet placed.

## Territory Rules

Every hex a party enters in the Faedom is **randomly selected** from this list — even if they've visited that hex before. The landscape shifts; nothing stays where you left it. Some hexes require a minimum depth (number of hexes traveled into the Faedom) before they can appear.

**Finding your way back:** Every POI has a way PCs can learn to summon it — a condition, a ritual, a relationship, or a piece of knowledge that lets them choose to encounter it again instead of rolling randomly.

**Fixed locations:** The Golden Oak and the Red Groves are the only POIs fixed to a specific hex. Everything else drifts.

| 4 | The Grove of Seasons | Time / Space | — | Four trees in a circle, each permanently in a different season — one in full bloom, one heavy with fruit, one bare, one frosted. Step between them and time skips. Useful, dangerous, unpredictable. |

| 1 | The Remembering Pool | Knowing / Wokn | — | A still, dark pool. Look into it and you see a memory you forgot you had. The memory is always true. The cost: the pool now knows it too, and Wokn's court can access it. *(Homestead sub-table)* |
| 16 | Entrance to a Fae Path | Passage | — | A threshold — an arch of twisted branches, a gap between two stones, a door-shaped shadow — that opens onto a Fae path. **Pocket path:** connects two specific POIs once opened. Where it leads depends on who's walking and what they carry. |
| 17 | The Green Lord's Tomb | Remnants of the War | 3 | A burial mound overgrown with moss and flowering vines. | 20 | The Hindsight Ring | Time / Space | 4 | A ring of mushrooms that, when sat within, lets you astral project into the past — observe but not interact. | 21 | The Homestead | Encounter / Hub | 1 | A cozy homestead with a welcome sign, smoke curling from the chimney, and three sub-POIs arranged in the yard: **Roll 3 from sub-table** (Remembering Pool, Singing Stones, Weeping Garden). A motherly Fae tends the place. If a PC hasn't tried at least one oddity she encourages them warmly — "Oh now, you really should. Try at least one, I always say." If they do, she makes a bed for them. If they refuse, she scolds. **Unlock:** Complete three of the sub-POIs → the hex can be summoned at the edge of an adjacent hex instead of appearing randomly. |
| 22 | The Snowdrift Cave | Mystery / Domain | — | A cave entrance half-buried beneath a permanent snowdrift — out of place, out of season. The air around it is winter-cold regardless of the weather. Inside is dark and still. **Pocket dimension:** The Bear of Winter's domain. |
| 23 | The Endless Masons | Time / Space | 3 | A time-compressed bubble surrounding a colony of quarter-inch Fae masons. Inside, hours pass while only minutes elapse outside. PCs could get significant work done here — crafting, planning, healing — while the world barely moves. The ground is a continuous city of tightly packed tiny buildings and spaces — dense enough that humans can walk on it like a platform, though you'd want to tread gently. Peering into the structures reveals great works: intricate architecture, spiraling buttresses, arched bridges, a three-story pyramid hundreds of years in the making. Thousands of masons, tireless, carrying grains of stone. They never stop. They never speak. They're not building toward anything — they're just *building*. They refuse all gifts — "too big." |
| 26 | The Amphitheatre | Remnants of the War | 4 | A stone amphitheatre with no one in attendance. On stage: stone statues of actors frozen mid-scene in a dramatic tableau. A vacant narrator's seat waits at the front. If someone watches — the statues return to "life," still stone-white but moving, enacting the play in silence. The scene depicts ancient history (the Green Lords' era). If someone sits in the narrator's seat and narrates while another watches — at last, curtain call. The statues bow, then wipe off white paint: they're actors. A Fae troupe, freed. They break camp cheerfully and offer PCs a ride with their wagon. Wherever you're headed? "It happens that's on the way!" |
| 27 | The Revel Clearing | Time / Space | 4 | A forest clearing lit by golden warmth and drifting motes. Fae of every kind gather here — horned drummers beating runed bodhráns, wild-haired pipers, tree-limbed dancers twisting in rings, tiny glowing sprites capering underfoot, a fiddler suspended in the canopy above. The music never stops; it just changes. PCs are always invited to dance, drum, drink, or simply watch. Time passes *fast* here — a single visit can burn a whole season without noticing. It never feels like loss; it feels like the best night of your life, repeated. **Unlock:** Pass four seasons here (four visits) → summonable at will. |
