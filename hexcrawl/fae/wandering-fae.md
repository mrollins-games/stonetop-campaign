# Fae Territory — Wandering Fae

Fae encountered while traveling through the territory. These are not tied to any POI — they may appear in any hex, as random encounters or drawn by circumstance.

| # | Name | Type | Summary |
|---|------|------|---------|
| 1 | The White Billies | Horde, athletic, raucous | Muscular folk with cloven hooves, birch-white fur, and horns. Endless revels — dancing, singing, drinking, brawling — to suppress their grief. Scarred war veterans who lash out if forced to face the past. Joining their revels is dangerous and costly. |
| 2 | Wee Folk | Horde, tiny, magical, stealthy | Tiny winged things — mishmashes of fur, feather, scales, gossamer. Some guard corrupted sites or Green Lord ruins. Others live in hidden cities (burrows, hollow trees). Others roam in cruel gangs looking for sport. Three instincts: guardian, worker, hooligan. |
| 3 | The Bloody Boots | Group, small, ugly, violent | A gang of hoodlums (Hobnail, Spitkicker, Big Thunk, Tusk, Grub). Short, squat brutes in oversized iron-shod boots. Feared war veterans. Bound by promise: they can't start fights — but they can finish them, and they'll enjoy it. |
| 4 | Blush-of-Dawn | Solitary, magical, beautiful | A radiant being, the beauty of sunrise made flesh. Bred to be adored by a Green Lord, now free but craving devotion. Inspires passion, jealousy, devotion. Steals away admirers to their domain — a pavilion forever at pre-dawn. |
| 5 | Grimstew | Solitary, small, tough, stealthy | Dwells in a cave of smoke and bones, tending a stew pot. Desperately lonely. Offers impossible labor in exchange for company. Grows enormous when angered. Eventually gets hungry and eats companions. |
| 6 | Hollow Fae | Solitary, undead, terrifying | A Fae wounded by black iron and cursed to un-life. A walking husk surrounded by death, draining life-force. Debts and promises no longer bind it. Only black iron destroys it. Other Fae flee or trick mortals into dealing with it. |
