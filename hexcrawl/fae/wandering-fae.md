# Fae — Creatures & NPCs

All Fae beings encountered in the Faedom. Some are tied to specific POIs, others wander freely.

| # | Name | Type | POI | Summary |
|---|------|------|-----|---------|
| 1 | The White Billies | Horde, athletic, raucous | — | Muscular folk with cloven hooves, birch-white fur, and horns. Endless revels — dancing, singing, drinking, brawling — to suppress their grief. Scarred war veterans who lash out if forced to face the past. Joining their revels is dangerous and costly. |
| 2 | Wee Folk | Horde, tiny, magical, stealthy | — | Tiny winged things — mishmashes of fur, feather, scales, gossamer. Some guard corrupted sites or Green Lord ruins. Others live in hidden cities (burrows, hollow trees). Others roam in cruel gangs looking for sport. Three instincts: guardian, worker, hooligan. |
| 3 | The Bloody Boots | Group, small, ugly, violent | — | A gang of hoodlums (Hobnail, Spitkicker, Big Thunk, Tusk, Grub). Short, squat brutes in oversized iron-shod boots. Feared war veterans. Bound by promise: they can't start fights — but they can finish them, and they'll enjoy it. |
| 4 | Blush-of-Dawn | Solitary, magical, beautiful | The Green Lord's Tomb | A radiant being, the beauty of sunrise made flesh. Bred to be adored by a Green Lord, now free but craving devotion. Inspires passion, jealousy, devotion. Steals away admirers to their domain — a pavilion forever at pre-dawn. |
| 5 | Grimstew | Solitary, small, tough, stealthy | — | Dwells in a cave of smoke and bones, tending a stew pot. Desperately lonely. Offers impossible labor in exchange for company. Grows enormous when angered. Eventually gets hungry and eats companions. |
| 6 | Hollow Fae | Solitary, undead, terrifying | — | A Fae wounded by black iron and cursed to un-life. A walking husk surrounded by death, draining life-force. Debts and promises no longer bind it. Only black iron destroys it. Other Fae flee or trick mortals into dealing with it. |
| 7 | Root-Keeper | Solitary, magical | — | Lives in hollow trees. Trades warmth and memories for secrets buried in the forest. Keeps "lost things" tangled in roots — objects, names, promises. What you trade away stays down there. |
| 8 | Thread-Bound | Solitary, bound | Liksa's Sunken Sette | A Fae sewn into a garment by Liksa's court. Unravel the thread, free the person. Whoever holds the spool controls them. They serve — hollow-eyed, obedient — until the thread is cut or the fabric unmade. |
| 9 | Canopy Hunter | Solitary, beast, predatory | — | Something in the high branches. More savage than anything at ground level. Drops, devours, gone. Deepwood only — never seen at shallow depths. No bargains. No warnings. Just silence, then weight from above. |
| 10 | Blight Sower | Solitary, parasitic | — | Corrupts growth itself. Gardens grow wrong, fruit tastes of ash, saplings twist into something else's shape. Parasitic — doesn't destroy, *replaces*. The new growth serves something. |
| 11 | The Oath-Keeper | Solitary, magical, tragic | — | Offers a lasting pact — protection, guidance, partnership — with impossible-seeming conditions. Generous, warm, genuine. Break one condition and they're gone. Irreversibly. No second chances. The loss is the point. |
| 12 | Aos Sí | Institution, noble host | — | A procession of the Old Court — grave-faced nobles in green and grey cloaks, bright hair, ancient finery. They travel between mounds along invisible roads. They don't attack. They *remind*. They arrive when an obligation has gone unmet. Each appearance is a grim portent: ignore them and things escalate. **Behavior:** 1st: silent procession at distance. 2nd: closer, one turns to look at the debtor. 3rd: they stop and wait. 4th: one speaks a single sentence naming the debt. 5th: consequences arrive without warning. Even Wokn pays them respect. |
| 13 | Unseelie Court | Institution, predatory host | — | Dark, ragged, or corpse-pale riders with black hounds and foul wind. They hunt stragglers — anyone separated from their group, lingering behind, or alone after dark. **Trigger:** separation from the party. If the separated PC rejoins before being caught, the hunt withdraws — but remembers. If caught alone: they offer a "ride" (conscription into the host for one night's hunt). Refusing means running. They don't kill — they *collect*. Makes "don't split the party" feel like Fae law. |

| 17 | The Nereids | Group, water fae | Hotsprings Rivera | Before and After — one community, different temperaments. See [POI file](../fae-pois/hotsprings-rivera.md). |
| 18 | The Ellyllon | Horde, tiny, mischievous | — (follow PCs on Depth 1) | Tiny goblin-fae with bright eyes and giggling voices. Stole an anchor stone. Can always be heard after discovery — giggling just out of sight, pilfering, staying barely out of reach. See [anchor stone mechanic](../fae-travel.md#the-ellyllon-and-the-anchor-stones). |
