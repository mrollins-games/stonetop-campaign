# The Warbler — Fae Chieftain of Spring

> One of the Four Chieftains, alongside the Bear of Winter, the Crowned Stag, and Longshadow.

## Stat Block

**THE WARBLER**
Solitary, fae, magical, stealthy, devious, shapechanger
HP 20; Armor 4 (resilience), 0 vs. iron
Damage: cutting song d10 (close, area, ignores armor) or talons d8 (hand)
Special qualities: Fae nature; shapechanging (bird forms, nondescript traveler); knows every secret spoken aloud beneath open sky; aura of birdsong

**Instinct:** to know everything worth knowing

**Moves:**
- Appear as a common bird, listening
- Appear as a forgettable traveler and strike up conversation
- Offer a secret in exchange for a secret (always comes out ahead)
- Fill the air with overwhelming song — distracting, enchanting, or deafening
- Call upon dozens of indebted Fae, each owing him "just a small thing"
- Reveal that he already knows what you came to ask
- Vanish mid-sentence, leaving only birdsong

## Narrative

The Warbler dwells in the springtime canopy. When the trees bud and the world wakes, his song fills the Great Wood — bright, layered, impossibly beautiful. Every bird that sings in his territory sings *for* him. They are his eyes, his ears, his couriers.

He was bred as a songbird for a Green Lord's pleasure garden — a living ornament. But he listened. He remembered everything spoken in his presence. When the rebellion came, he was the one who carried messages between cells, who coordinated the timing of uprisings across a dozen domains. Not a fighter. Never a fighter. The one who made sure the right people were in the right place at the right time.

After the war, he leveraged what he knew. Every secret overhead, every promise witnessed, every lie detected — all of it became currency. He is owed more favors than perhaps any other Fae alive. Not because he's powerful in the way the Bear is powerful. Because he knows things. And because refusing the Warbler means he might *share* what he knows.

He pretends to be a fool. Chatters endlessly. Gossips about nothing. Laughs too easily. It's an act so perfect that even Fae who know it's an act still underestimate him. He goes incognito constantly — as a wren on a branch, as a shabby peddler, as a bored courier resting at a waystone. He is *extremely* well-informed.

## Personality & Demeanor

- **In bird form:** Unremarkable. A wren, a thrush, a warbler. Hops close. Tilts its head. Listens.
- **In traveler form:** A slight, plain-faced person. Forgettable clothes. Easy smile. Asks too many questions but makes it feel like friendly conversation. Laughs at his own jokes.
- **When revealed:** The air fills with layered birdsong from every direction. His eyes sharpen. He speaks with absolute precision — every word chosen, every pause deliberate. Still smiling.
- **Angered:** He doesn't rage. He goes quiet. The birdsong stops. Everything stops. Then he tells you something about yourself you never wanted anyone to know.

---

## Relationships

- **The Bear of Winter:** Respects his brute power. They trade favors carefully. The Warbler knows things about the Bear that keep their relationship civil.
- **The Crowned Stag:** Finds him charming but reckless. Often cleans up after the Stag's romantic entanglements.
- **Longshadow:** The closest thing he has to an equal. They share information. He suspects she's planning something vast and is content to watch it unfold.
- **Lord Wokn:** An interesting new player. The Warbler is *fascinated* by Wokn's "knowing" mechanism — it rhymes with his own methods. Unclear if they're rivals or natural allies.

---

## What He Wants

1. **Information.** Always. About everything. Especially things people don't want known.
2. **Influence without visibility.** He doesn't want a throne or a court. He wants to be the one whispered to before decisions are made.
3. **Entertainment.** Mortals are *fascinating*. So dramatic. So short-lived. So careless with their secrets.

---

## How PCs Might Encounter Him

- A friendly traveler at the Courier's Rest or the Performer's Rest, asking innocent questions
- A bird that keeps appearing wherever they go, always just within earshot
- A Fae who owes him a debt sends the PCs to deliver a message (the message *is* the payment)
- He approaches directly if they possess information he can't get elsewhere — rare, and flattering
