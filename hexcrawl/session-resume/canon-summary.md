## The Big Picture

### The Broken Contract
- Stonetop's ancestors made a pact with the Fae: **never fell the trees of the Great Wood**
- The trees are prisons — they BIND corrupted Forest Folk spirits inside them
- Felling a tree = freeing a corrupted spirit (this is what happened at C7)
- The Fae enforced the contract to protect the prisons they created

### What Happened to the Forest Folk
- During the Cataclysm, the **Green Lords** called upon Things Below to corrupt the Forest Folk
- Purpose: to weaponize them against the Fae (who had become "traitorous")
- ALL Forest Folk were turned — twisted by Things Below corruption
- The Fae WON: they imprisoned the corrupted Forest Folk in the trees
- This answers "what happened to the Forest Folk" — they're all still here, bound in wood
- Open question: WHY did the Fae bind rather than destroy?

### The Corruption Thread (Daagon)
- **D10 (Dark Pool)**: Source. Tendril of Daagon, Who Waits in Deep Waters. Burrowed up through riverbed.
- **B10 (The Maw)**: Same fissure, surface opening. Connected underground to D10.
- River carries corruption DOWNSTREAM: D10 → C9 → B8 → C7 → D6 → D4 → E3 → F2 → G1
- **Fish are corrupted** everywhere downstream of D10 (milky eyes, extra fins, aggressive)
- **Forest Folk solution** (now defunct): Cistern at C9 purified water, weir at B8 caught fish before they reached the deep. System failed when Forest Folk were corrupted.

### Pre-Corruption Relations
- Forest Folk and Fae had cautious coexistence — "Good Neighbors," "Hidden Ones"
- Iron hurts Fae; they value debts and promises
- The Green Lords' betrayal broke this peace

### The Hunting Route: D8 → E7 → E5
The main hunting corridor from Stonetop into the Great Wood. Dilwen's traditional trapping route. Filberta grew up on it.

An underground current of energy flowing from C9 (Forest Folk outpost) through the hunting route, crossing the river at E5, and arriving at Stonetop. Gareth (Azure Hand) can sense this passively.

## Key Canon Decisions

### The Halfeyd & Hunter Offerings (Book II p.371)
- When you make a kill, you leave an offering so you don't become prey
- The Halfeyd marks those who kill without offering — hunted by all predators for 3 days
- Running water washes the mark away
- **This is hunter in-group knowledge** — passed down generation to generation. It's what defines hunters as a distinct group. Non-hunters don't know about it.
- Townsfolk just accept "hunters hunt, non-hunters don't" as unwritten law
- **Dilwen traps instead of hunts** — his loophole. The animals die when he isn't there. He THINKS this means he doesn't have to make the offering.
- **Filberta knows the offering** (learned from other hunters). She finds Dilwen's loophole repelling — cowardly, disrespectful.

### Sunlit Hollow (E7, POI 3)
- Sacred site where Helior and Danu overlap
- All 5 manifestations combined: canopy parts for light, warmth in soil, golden hour persists, bees glow, sunstone present
- **Honey**: luminescent, warming, cures cold-related ailments ONLY (not general healing). Persists anywhere Helior or Danu hold influence.
- **Sunstone**: physical manifestation of Helior/Danu coincidence. Not a spirit — an object.
- **Bee breadcrumb in D8**: a single bee lands on Halpin (connects to Helior)

### The Squirrel Colony (E7, POI 4)
- Relay communication network — directional chain, like telephone poles
- Avoids ALL humans (not just Dilwen) — disperses from whoever is near
- Filberta's squirrel is a former colony member who left to follow her
- Her squirrel comes home CALMLY here (not manic — this is home)

### The Drowned Girl Spirit (E5 / D4)
- Ghost anchored at the E5 ford crossing
- Guides swimmers safely — no one has ever drowned at this ford
- Can appear at D4 side too (across the river)
- No one knows she's there

### The Ford Crossing (E5 → D4)
- Genuinely dangerous: chest-high summer, neck-high spring. Never calm — sometimes calmer.
- Vicious undertow, uneven riverbed, algae-slick rocks
- Townsfolk won't attempt it. Wild-headed think they could manage on a calm-ish day.
- Defy Danger even WITH the spirit helping (she reduces lethality, doesn't eliminate danger)

### Dilwen — "Never There"
- He is never there when the animal dies (trapping loophole)
- He is never there in the broader sense — leaving Filberta behind, leaving camp before dawn, always somewhere else when things happen
- This is a thematic through-line for the character

- Severely corrupted fish. Bare banks. Cold water. Night whispers.
- Swimming/wading triggers the emanation's attention

### E7 — The Food Nexus
Full file: `hexcrawl/hex-e7.md` (5 POIs, most complex hex)
1. **The Food Nexus** — Forest Folk cultivated coppice. Oaks/beeches/hazel in deliberate patterns. Ley line feeds root network. Game converges.
2. **The Restless Oak** (NE edge, bordering F8) — Uncorrupted Forest Folk spirit merged naturally with her tree in death. Speaks to Wolfram via Spirit Tongue.
3. **The Sunlit Hollow** — Helior/Danu convergence. Luminescent bees, sunstone, golden honey.
4. **The Canopy Parliament** — Squirrel colony with relay communication. Filberta's squirrel comes home here.
5. **The Cusp** (NE edge, bordering F8) — Jade wave Fae boundary markers. Projection point of binding spell.

### E5 — The Welcome Glade
Full file: `hexcrawl/hex-e5.md` (3 POIs)
1. **The Glade & Trail Spirit** — Lightning-bolt shaped clearing (widest SW at river, narrows/zig-zags N). The glade IS the ley line's surface expression — the energy burned absence in tree growth over centuries. Trail spirit makes it feel welcoming.
2. **The Crossing** — Dangerous river ford (see canon above). Drowned girl spirit guides swimmers.
3. **Dilwen's Camp** — Hazel bower in the narrow northern zig-zag. Hazel is unnaturally bright yellow (ley energy concentrating at tip). Visible from Stonetop cliff as "sparks at end of lightning strike." Trap-line runs N into E7.
- **Dilwen's party is HERE** (3 people total). Storm has them hunkered down.
