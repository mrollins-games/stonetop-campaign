## POI 1: The Rooted Pass

**Description:**
> A steep hillside, almost a wall of earth, 95% covered in thick exposed tree roots — gnarled, interlocking, ancient. In the rain they're slick as ice, black with moisture. The gaps between roots drop into unseen tangles below. There is no clear path unless you know where to look.

**Hazard:**

| Danger | Trigger | Consequence |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| Slick root traverse | Climbing down/up without knowing the route | Defy Danger +DEX. Miss: fall, d6 damage, gear caught/lost in root tangle. 7-9: make it but twisted ankle, torn pack, or noise draws attention below. |

**Filberta (Kari):** Advantage on Defy Danger here. She's done this a dozen times on trapping trips Dilwen dragged her on. She knows which roots hold, which are rotten, where to place feet. She can guide others (Aid with advantage, or narrate the route so others roll normally instead of blind).

**Squirrel at the Rooted Pass:**
- This is squirrel highway — it's been here hundreds of times. Runs ahead, chittering back, showing the route.
- Knows what nests in the root tangles — warns of snakes, wasp nests, fox dens.
- Knows what's at the bottom TODAY — if feathered drakes are near the base, it freezes, tail puffed, staring down. That's Filberta's warning.

**Discoveries:**

| # | Discovery | Move/Trigger |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | The roots belong to the managed trees below — the system extends up the hillside | Free (obvious on close look) |
| 2 | An old snare anchor hammered into a root — Dilwen's work, from a trip years ago | Filberta recognizes it (free) |

## POI 2: The Hunting Party's Canopy Escape

**Description:**
> At the base of the Rooted Pass, the terrain levels into the edge of ancient woodland. Massive oaks and beeches, thinning understory, trunks spaced wider. Ahead, through the trees, a clearing opens — the edge of E7's Food Nexus.

> But something is wrong. Feathered drakes — three, maybe four — pace between the trunks below, heads swivelling, crests raised. They shouldn't be here. They nest deeper east. Something pushed them into this corridor.

> High above, lashed to the branches of a broad oak: a hunter's platform. A hide tarp rigged against rain. Quiet. Someone is up there.

**Hywel's Party:**
- Platform is 30+ feet up. Old construction — lashed branches, vine-bound joints, no nails. (Possibly Forest Folk origin; hunters just think "someone built this ages ago.")
- **Hywel** (eldest, leader), **Eirlys**, and **Cadoc** — party of 3.
- Dry but bored, low on food, nervous. Up here two days.
- Trapped by: storm swelled B8's ford (can't get home), drakes appeared below, boar sow is aggressive.
- They'll call down quietly if they recognize Stonetop folk: "Psst! Up here! Don't move fast — there's drakes."

**Feathered Drakes (3-4):**
- Group, stealthy, organized. HP 6, Armor 1.
- Damage: boney crest d8+2 (close, forceful) / talon & teeth d8+2 (hand, messy, 1 piercing)
- Instinct: to spring on unwary prey.
- Displaced from normal nesting area (deeper east). Why? — left open for now.
- Hunting the same boar the party was after. Patient — circling, waiting.

**Boar sow with piglets:**
- Territorial. Blocks the direct path south toward E7 clearing edge.
- HP 10, Armor 0. Damage: gore d8 (hand, messy, 1 piercing). Instinct: to protect territory.
- Won't stop charging unless driven off or evaded.

**Discoveries:**

| # | Discovery | Move/Trigger |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Drakes are displaced — their nesting ground is east (E7/E9 border). Something pushed them here. | Free observation / Know Things for why |
| 2 | The platform is old — older than anyone in Stonetop. Vine-bound, notched joints, no rope or nails. | Seek Insight 7-9 |
| 3 | From the canopy, looking south into E7: the Food Nexus pattern is unmistakable — regular tree spacing, species mix, converging game trails. It's designed. | Free (visible from height) |

**Dangers:**

| Danger | Trigger | Consequence |

| Feathered drakes (3-4) | Moving through open ground without stealth/distraction | They stalk, then burst from brush. d8+2, forceful. Pack tactics — surround, bash prone, rend. |
