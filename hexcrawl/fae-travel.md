# Faedom Travel Mechanics

## The Journeyman's Trail

- There is a journeyman's trail/road through the Faedom
- **Stay on it** to avoid the more extreme manifestations
- Two pairs of Standing Stones anchor the trail (four total) — they anchor the trail to a place, then reverse the place to the trail

## The Ellyllon and the Anchor Stones

**Trigger:** The first time PCs reach Depth Two (go deep), they discover their path home can't be found.

**What happened:**
- One anchor stone is easily visible — just 30–60 feet out of its place, clearly moved
- The other has been taken by **Ellyllon** — tiny goblin-fae with bright eyes and giggling voices
- They can be heard just off the trail in the woods, laughing, luring

**Once discovered:**
- The Ellyllon can *always* be heard from then on — giggling just out of sight
- They follow the PCs from POI to POI on Depth 1
- They never attack, never confront — just giggle, pilfer, and stay barely out of reach
- The missing stone is the reason PCs can't simply walk home; recovering or replacing it is the key to restoring the path

> The Ellyllon are persistent, uncanny, and deeply annoying. They've made the Faedom slightly harder to leave — not as a trap, but because they think it's funny.

## Depth System

Traveling in the Faedom takes you places — but **not always the same place**. POIs are randomized each time a PC enters a hex.

### Rules
- Each POI can only appear at certain **depths**
- Ask players: do you want to go **deeper**, **back (shallower)**, or **to the side**?
- Or infer from their hex choice — keep track of their depth
- Navigation: Deeper = South, Back (shallower) = North, Sideways = East/West

### Fae Directions

Fae can give directions and they are of some use:
- Wherever you want to go, they will say it's **"near X"** — where X is a POI in the depth column you want to reach
- If the destination is **two columns away**, they say **"go past Y, then it's near X"** — where Y is a POI they have visited in the in-between column (this column ± 1)
