# Hex E7 — The Food Nexus

**Terrain:** Ancient managed woodland. Forest Folk cultivated coppice.
**Visible?** NO (pattern only visible from within or from canopy height)
**Hunting route:** D8 → E7 → E9 (the main hunting corridor from Stonetop)
**Note:** The Food Nexus takes up most of this hex. Multiple POIs exist within it.

## Entry Description

> The forest changes. The undergrowth thins and massive trunks rise in patterns that feel — wrong? No. Deliberate. Oaks, beeches, hazel — all growing in complementary layers, spaced too evenly to be chance. The ground is carpeted in acorn caps, beech husks, hazelnut shells. Game trails converge from every direction. Boar root contentedly. Deer tracks everywhere. The forest PROVIDES here, and has for longer than anyone remembers.

## POI 1: The Food Nexus

**Description:**
> A broad stretch of ancient woodland where trees stand in deliberate patterns — massive oaks, stately beeches, hazel understory filling the middle layer. Multiple mast species coexisting where they normally wouldn't. Game trails converge toward the centre like spokes on a wheel, worn deep into soil. This place was designed to attract life — and it still works.

**The Ley Line & Root Network:**
A ley line runs from the Forest Folk outpost at C9 through D8 and into E7. It carries purifying energy — the same magic that powered the cistern's ward-glyphs at C9. Here at the nexus, that energy feeds the root network, making the managed plantings unnaturally vigorous.

The cycle (now broken):
1. **Ley line** carries purifying energy from C9 → D8 → E7
2. Energy makes roots flourish, drives the cultivation network (trees produce abundantly)
3. Forest Folk hunted here, brought food back to C9
4. Their presence and rites at C9 sustained the ley line in return

With the Forest Folk gone, the ley line still flows (it's ancient, pre-dates them) but weakly. The cultivation persists on momentum — slowly fading over decades. The root network is alive but no longer actively maintained.

**Signs of Intentional Cultivation:**

| # | Discovery | Move/Trigger |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Trees spaced too evenly. Multiple mast species coexisting unnaturally. | Free (obvious from platform in D8 or walking through) |
| 2 | Faint knife-scars at tree bases — old girdling marks where Forest Folk thinned competitors. Healed over but visible. | Seek Insight 7-9 |
| 3 | Ground flora is suspiciously useful — wild garlic, wood sorrel, mushroom logs that seem deliberately placed. | Forage (advantage here) or Seek Insight |
| 4 | Root network visible where rain eroded soil — roots from different species are GRAFTED together underground. Unnatural. Intentional. | Seek Insight 10+ or Wolfram Vessel sense (free) |
| 5 | Ley line node — a point where energy concentrates. Marked by a ring of mushrooms (fairy ring) growing year-round. Ground is warmer here. | Gareth Azure Hand (free) or Seek Insight 10+ |

**Know Things results:**

A ley line feeds it from the north. The Forest Folk hunted here and brought food back to their outpost. The system outlived them." |

**Forage:** Advantage in this hex. The place is designed to provide.

**Filberta at the Food Nexus:**
- She's ALWAYS known this place is special. She's foraged here before.

- If she pieces together the cultivation system, that's a Potential for Greatness moment — connecting to the Forest Folk legacy through the thing she does best.
- Squirrel leads her to cached hazelnuts — free provisions (1-2 uses, no roll). "It digs at the base of a hazel, chattering. A cache — more than one squirrel could have buried."

## POI 2: The Restless Oak

**Location:** Eastern edge of the Food Nexus, bordering Fae territory (E9).

**Description:**
> A broad ancient oak, larger than its neighbours but not impossibly so. Bark deeply furrowed. Roots grip the earth in thick coils, grafted into the root network. In the rain, sap weeps from cracks in patterns too regular to be natural — like tears tracing the same channels over and over.

**The Spirit Within — Her Story:**
She was a guardian-tender of the Food Nexus in life. A Forest Folk woman who spent her years cultivating this system, connected deeply to the ley line and root network.

When the Green Lords betrayed the Forest Folk and called upon Things Below to corrupt them — the purifying energy of the ley line and root network protected her. She was never corrupted.

She was not forcibly bound by the Fae. The Fae binding swept through indiscriminately, but it didn't matter for her — she wasn't corrupted, wasn't a threat. She died naturally, her body decomposed into the soil, and her spirit joined with the oak organically. A natural spiritual merging with the tree she'd tended for a lifetime.

**This is not a prison. This is her home.**

**Key distinction:**
- Other bound Forest Folk = corrupted spirits forced into tree-prisons by the Fae
- The Restless Oak = uncorrupted spirit who merged naturally with her tree in death

**Why she's restless:**
- The ley line is weakening without Forest Folk tending the cycle
- She senses the corruption downstream growing (Daagon's tendril at D10)
- The system she built is slowly failing
- She's been trying to reach someone for generations

**Communication:**
Direct voice in Wolfram's mind only (Spirit Tongue). Old voice, tired, like wind through dry leaves. Only he hears it. Others see a tree doing nothing. Speaks in fragments, long pauses — hasn't spoken in a very long time.

**Discoveries:**

| # | Discovery | Move/Trigger |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Sap weeps from bark cracks in repeating patterns — same channels, over and over, like the tree is trying to show something | Seek Insight 7-9 |
| 2 | Roots shift subtly when you stand near — pointing toward the Food Nexus centre, then toward C9, tracing a connection | Wolfram Spirit Tongue (free) or Seek Insight 10+ |
| 3 | No Fae binding marks. No rune-carved roots wrapping dark shapes. The energy here is warm, not caged. This isn't a prison. | Gareth Azure Hand (free) |
| 4 | A hollow at head-height with a smooth warm river-stone inside. No carvings. Placed there on purpose, long ago. | Physical search (free) |

**What she knows / might say (via Wolfram):**
- The cultivation system and how it worked
- That the corruption came from the river, not the roots
- That the Fae bound everyone — "all my kin. Taken into the wood. I heard them scream."
- That she wasn't taken because she was already part of the tree
- That the ley line is weakening — "the flow thins. Roots forget."
- She doesn't know what year it is or how long it's been

**What she doesn't know:**
- What happened after the binding (she's been here the whole time)
- What Stonetop is or that humans live nearby
- Whether any other uncorrupted Forest Folk exist

**Squirrel at the Restless Oak:**
- Calm here. Sits on the roots contentedly. Has done this before — this tree was always a good resting spot.
- Filberta never knew why it felt safe.
