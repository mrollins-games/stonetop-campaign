# Art Asset Index

> Extracted from Stonetop game PDFs. Use for slide decks, handouts, Copilot Studio, or any visual materials.
>
> **⚠️ Before generating any art, read `style-guide.md` in this folder.** It defines the canonical art style AND physical features of Stonetop to prevent off-canon depictions.
> 
> **Fonts used in source material:**
> - **Headings:** Avara Bold (geometric serif)
> - **Body:** Historical Fell Type Roman SC (available as "IM Fell English" on Google Fonts)
> 
> **Art Style:** Hand-drawn ink illustrations, woodcut-inspired, earthy/grounded. Black ink on cream/white. Occasional spot color (amber, red-brown).

## Notes for Other Agents

- **Font pairing:** Use "IM Fell English" (Google Fonts) for body text, "Avara Bold" or "Josefin Slab Bold" for headings
- **Background color:** Off-white parchment `#F5F0E8`
- **Art style for generation:** Hand-drawn ink illustration, woodcut-inspired, black ink on cream. No digital gradients. Cross-hatching for shading. Earthy, grounded, Iron Age fantasy.
- **All images are PNG format**, rendered at 2x PDF scale
- **Maps are landscape** (wider than tall); Book I pages are portrait
- **Copyright:** These are extracted from purchased game PDFs for personal campaign use only. Do not redistribute.
