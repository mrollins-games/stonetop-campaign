# Stonetop Visual Style Guide

> **Purpose:** Give this document to any AI art generator, human artist, or agent producing visual content for this campaign. It defines the canonical art style AND the physical/geographic features of Stonetop to prevent off-canon depictions.

---

## How to Use This Guide

1. **Read this entire document** before generating any art.
2. **Find reference images:** The art index is at `assets/art/_art-index.md`. It contains tagged entries for every extracted piece of source art.
3. **Pick 2-4 reference images** with tags relevant to what you're creating (e.g., generating a forest scene → look at images tagged `forest`, `great-wood`, `dark`). View those images to calibrate your style and physical details.
4. **Art files live in:** `assets/art/` with subfolders:
   - `assets/art/maps/` — Village, vicinity, world maps
   - `assets/art/scenes/` — Book illustrations, atmosphere, chapter art
   - `assets/art/characters/` — Playbook class art (front/back pages)
   - `assets/art/items/` — Gear, equipment, trade illustrations
   - `assets/art/decorative/` — Borders, dividers
5. **Match style to source.** Your output should look like it belongs in the same book as the reference images.

### Reference Workflow for Agents
```
1. Identify what you're drawing (village? forest? character? monster?)
2. Open assets/art/_art-index.md
3. Find 2-4 images with matching tags
4. View those images to study: line weight, shading technique, composition, physical details
5. Generate art matching that style and those physical features
6. Compare your output against the references — does it look like it belongs in the same book?
```

## Part 1: Art Style

### Medium & Technique
- **Medium:** Black ink on white/cream paper
- **Technique:** Hand-drawn illustration with pen and ink
- **Shading:** Cross-hatching and stippling (NO smooth gradients, NO digital airbrush)
- **Line weight:** Variable — heavier outlines, finer detail lines. Confident, slightly rough strokes.
- **Style influences:** Woodcut prints, medieval manuscript illustrations, 1970s-80s RPG illustration (Russ Nicholson, Dave Trampier)
- **Mood:** Earthy, grounded, tactile. NOT polished, NOT digital-looking, NOT anime/manga
- **Composition:** Often vignettes in circles or bordered panels. Landscape panoramas for headers. Dense detail in focal areas, looser/sparser at edges.

### What the Art LOOKS Like
- Black ink dominant — minimal or no color
- Textures are drawn (hatched grass, stippled stone, cross-hatched shadows)
- Skies filled with layered pen strokes suggesting clouds/weather
- Trees are organic, gnarled, with individually-drawn leaves or branch patterns
- Architecture is rough, low, asymmetric — NOT symmetrical or polished
- Figures are realistic proportions, posed naturally (not heroic/dynamic poses)
- Overall feel: like looking at a skilled field sketch or archaeological illustration

### What to AVOID
- ❌ Digital painting / smooth gradients / airbrush effects
- ❌ Bright saturated colors (if color is used at all, it should be muted earth tones)
- ❌ Clean vector lines / geometric precision
- ❌ Anime/manga style
- ❌ High-fantasy glamor (no glowing runes, no particle effects)
- ❌ Modern materials or techniques in the world (no cut stone blocks, no glass windows)
- ❌ Anything that looks "rendered" or 3D
- ❌ Over-the-top heroic poses or exaggerated proportions

### Typography (for documents/slides)
- **Headings:** Avara Bold (geometric serif) — substitute: Josefin Slab Bold
- **Body text:** Historical Fell Type Roman SC — substitute: IM Fell English (Google Fonts, free)
- **Chapter headers in the books** use a large decorative serif with slight irregularity
- **Background color:** Off-white parchment `#F5F0E8`
- **Accent color:** Deep mossy slate green `#2D4A3E`
- **Text color:** Near-black `#1A1A1A`

## Part 2: Stonetop — Physical Canon

### The Village Layout (CRITICAL — most common error)

**Stonetop is NOT:**
- ❌ A walled medieval town
- ❌ A stone/masonry settlement
- ❌ A village with timber-frame or half-timbered buildings
- ❌ A compact clustered village
- ❌ Anything with cobblestone streets, towers, or keep

**Stonetop IS:**
- A loose cluster of **low, squat buildings** on a raised bluff
- Buildings are made of **rough stone scavenged from the crumbling Old Wall** — irregular fieldstone, NOT cut blocks
- Roofs are **thatched with barley straw** — thick, sloped, golden-brown
- Buildings are passed down 2-3 generations, expanded organically
- The overall impression is: Iron Age Celtic hillfort, NOT medieval European village
- Think: Skara Brae (Orkney), Irish ring forts, early Iron Age Scottish settlements

### Geography & Terrain

**The Bluff:**
- Stonetop sits on a **raised bluff/plateau** — elevated above surrounding terrain
- The bluff overlooks **the Great Wood** to the east/southeast (dense forest stretching to the horizon)
- The **Stream** runs along the base of the bluff — shallow fresh water (but people fear deep water)
- South/southwest of the bluff: **the Fields** (cultivated farmland stretching to the Old Wall)
- The terrain drops away on at least the eastern side toward the forest

**Surrounding landscape:**
- **The Flats** (west/southwest) — wide open prairie between Great Wood and Huffel Peaks. Weedy grasses, flat, lonely. Aurochs, deer, wolves, cougars roam here.
- **The Foothills / Huffel Peaks** (west/northwest) — rising terrain toward mountains. Timber comes from here (not the Great Wood — they never cut living trees from it).
- **The Great Wood** (east/southeast) — dense, old-growth deciduous forest. Dark canopy, undergrowth, ancient. NOT a pine/conifer forest. Oaks, beeches, potentially some birch.
- **Mountains** visible in the distance to the north and west

**Roads:**
- **The West Road** — Maker-built, impossibly well-preserved stone road running from Stonetop west to Gordin's Delve
- **The Highway** — crosses the West Road, runs north from Barrier Pass toward Marshedge and beyond
- **The Crossroads** — where West Road meets the Highway, south of Stonetop

### The Stone (CRITICAL — second most common error)

**The Stone is NOT:**
- ❌ A perfect obelisk or geometric shape
- ❌ A Stonehenge-style dolmen or trilithon
- ❌ Glowing, magical-looking, or covered in visible luminous runes
- ❌ Small (it's over 50 feet tall)
- ❌ Dark/black stone
- ❌ Part of a circle of stones

**The Stone IS:**
- A single **massive standing stone (menhir)**, over 50 feet tall
- **Rough, natural-looking** — irregular surface, weathered
- Covered in **faded, barely-visible runes/spiral markings** etched into the surface — they're OLD, worn smooth by time. You have to look closely to see them.
- The carvings visible in canon art show **circular spiral patterns/cup marks** (like Neolithic petroglyphs — NOT angular Norse runes)
- **Taller than any building** — dominates the village skyline
- Appears to be **a single piece** — not stacked or constructed
- **Lightning strikes it constantly** during storms (but no visible damage or scorch marks — it absorbs it)
- Located roughly **centrally** in the village (see map: labeled "A")
- It is **older than the Maker ruins** the village is built on — which are themselves ancient
- Color: grey/brown weathered stone, similar to local fieldstone but different in character

### The Old Wall

- A **massive, crumbling stone rampart** that once encircled an area about a mile out from the Stone
- Clearly **giant-sized construction** (Maker-built) — individual blocks are enormous
- Now mostly collapsed/buried — villagers **quarry it for building stone**
- NOT intact — it's rubble, buried sections, occasional standing segments
- The Fields extend from village center to the Old Wall

### The Ringwall

- A **waist-high fieldstone wall** around the village core
- Built by villagers from scavenged Old Wall stone
- NOT defensive against armies — symbolic boundary + keeps livestock in
- Newer homes are built **outside** the Ringwall

### Key Buildings

**The Public House:**
- The largest building in town
- Where everyone gathers after sundown to socialize
- Offers floor space for travelers and a stable for the town's two horses
- Built from the same scavenged stone + thatch as everything else, just bigger

**The Granary:**
- Communal food storage
- Important enough to be marked on the map

**The Pavilion of the Gods:**
- An **open-air** shrine area (NOT a temple building)
- Hosts shrines to four gods: Tor, Helior, Danu, Aratis
- Think: a covered outdoor space with separate shrine alcoves or standing stones/carved posts for each god

**The Cistern:**
- Ancient Maker-built underground water storage
- Still functional despite being older than the village
- Fed by rainwater and snowmelt

**Three Watchtowers:**
- Simple wooden watchtowers at the village perimeter
- Manned nightly by militia rotation
- NOT stone towers — wooden platforms

### Climate & Season

- **Climate:** Temperate, four distinct seasons. Similar to northern Britain/Scotland.
- **Spring:** Wildflowers in the Flats, trees budding in the Great Wood
- **Summer:** Warm but not hot. Frequent storms (lightning striking the Stone)
- **Autumn:** Harvest season. The Red Grove turns blood-red.
- **Winter:** Cold, snow likely. The Stream may dry up in summer but flows in winter/spring.

### People & Culture

- **Population:** ~300 souls
- **Ethnicity:** Not specified — can be diverse. Names suggest mixed Celtic/Germanic/Mediterranean influences.
- **Clothing:** Practical, homespun. Wool, linen, leather. NO silk, NO fine fabrics. Think: early medieval peasant dress, possibly with Bronze/Iron Age elements (torcs, brooches, woad)
- **Armor:** Leather, bronze, iron. Round wooden shields. Spears as primary weapons. Some bows. Good steel is RARE.
- **Technology level:** Iron Age. No windmills, no watermills (they fear deep water), no complex machinery. Hand tools, animal labor (2 horses, goats, chickens, sheep).

### The Great Wood (for forest scenes)

- **Type:** Dense, old-growth deciduous forest (NOT conifer/pine)
- **Canopy:** Thick, filtering light to dappled patches on the forest floor
- **Undergrowth:** Moderate — ferns, mosses, fallen logs
- **Character:** Ancient, slightly oppressive, beautiful but dangerous
- **Inhabitants:** Crinwin (small grey-skinned mimics), Fae, cave bears, wolves, deer, cougars, drakes
- **The Red Grove:** A specific stand where ALL vegetation turns blood red in autumn (leaves, bark, everything)
- The Forest Folk (now vanished) lived here — they were NOT human (strange peoples)

## Part 3: Prompt Templates

### For village scenes:
> "Black ink illustration, hand-drawn, cross-hatched shading. An Iron Age village of low squat stone buildings with thick thatched straw roofs, built on a raised bluff. A massive 50-foot standing stone with faded spiral carvings dominates the center. Dense old-growth forest visible in the background below the bluff. Waist-high fieldstone wall encircles the settlement. No color, pen and ink style, woodcut-inspired."

### For landscape/wilderness:
> "Black ink illustration, pen and ink, cross-hatched shading. [Describe scene]. Temperate climate, deciduous old-growth forest, rolling hills, ancient overgrown stone roads. Style: detailed naturalistic field sketch, woodcut-inspired. No color."

### For characters/people:
> "Black ink illustration, hand-drawn. [Character description]. Wearing practical homespun clothing, wool and leather. Iron Age technology — bronze jewelry, iron tools, wooden shields. Natural poses, realistic proportions. Cross-hatched shading, pen and ink style."

### For monsters/dangers:
> "Black ink illustration, dense cross-hatching. [Creature/danger description]. Dark, oppressive atmosphere. Heavy shadows, minimal background detail. Horror and wonder mixed — not cartoonish. Pen and ink, woodcut-inspired. No color."

## Part 4: Common Errors to Correct

| Error | What agents generate | What it should be |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Buildings | Cut-stone masonry, half-timber, tall | Rough fieldstone, low/squat, thick thatch |
| The Stone | Geometric obelisk, glowing runes, dark stone | Rough natural menhir, faded spirals, weathered grey |
| Architecture | Medieval European, walled town | Iron Age hillfort, organic, no formal walls |
| Forest | Pine/conifer, sparse | Dense old-growth deciduous, thick canopy |
| Technology | Medieval+ (windmills, glass, crossbows) | Iron Age (hand tools, spears, round shields, bronze) |
| People | Medieval peasants or high-fantasy heroes | Iron Age villagers, practical dress, homespun |
| Terrain | Flat village on a plain | Village on a raised bluff/plateau |
| Color | Full-color painted | Black ink on white, no color |
| Style | Digital painting, smooth rendering | Hand-drawn ink, cross-hatching, woodcut feel |
| Scale | Stone is small, human-height | Stone is 50+ feet tall, dominates skyline |
